# Captain Juno Pell, courier

*Contracts.* Event: `distress` (DISTRESS CALL). Minigame: `tow`. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A courier adrift, engines dead, air for a day, with a warm crate sealed by [the Syndicate](../factions/syndicate.md) that she's not to open.

- **Fronts:** tow her clear (scrap, Port Authority +1, a part on the bold tow); carry her crate to a far planet before the gate (+90, the run's contract); or take the crate (+70, a hunt, bounty +1).
- **Quirk:** pays "probably". Calls you names you have to look up when the cable snaps.

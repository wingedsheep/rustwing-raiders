# Tobbo Grisk, junk dealer

*Proposed* (style guide §3.10). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A pot-bellied alien hovering on buzzing wings over a yard of parts. Fast-talking, wheedling, insulted by low offers, cheats at odds.

- **Would front:** haggling: every part has a hidden real price, and pushing too hard closes the shop for the visit. Sometimes a bet instead of a sale. On Kessra and Ashgrave.
- **Voice:** raspy, sing-song, Grisk sound-set (`Bahwoh-zza! Zzimbo, zzimbo wahh!`), with a wing-buzz layer. The worked example in `docs/alien-voices.md` §7.
- [ ] His bet was "a race on par"; racing is cut.

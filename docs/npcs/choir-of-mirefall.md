# The Choir of Mirefall

*Proposed* (style guide §3.5). [The Hive](../factions/the-hive.md). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A fungal mind grown through a crashed freighter, speaking through its intercom. Eerie, not funny: always "we", polite, slightly wrong. In the lore it's the largest **nemva** there is: the bloom, which learnt from the crew of the *Tamsin Lowe* ([minds](../lore/minds.md#nemva-you-can-meet)).

- **Would front:** parts that grow stronger each jump but slowly turn a neighbour bloom. Taking them raises your mood with the Hive and makes the others wary. On Mirefall.
- **Voice:** English, through a crackling intercom, three detuned layers. A hummed chord with static.

# Rollo Stax, the voice of the lanes

*News.* Event: `gazette` (THE LANTERN GAZETTE). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A pirate broadcaster. "The only news that isn't lying to you on purpose!" His news moves markets.

- **Fronts:** market news (a dealer pays ×3 for one class of part this sector); for 40, report you dead (bounty gone, and Kaspar hears it too); give him a story (+15, the planet's faction −1 mood).
- **Quirk:** always on air. "...tragically lost with all hands. A moment's silence. That's enough."

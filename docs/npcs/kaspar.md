# Kaspar, who hunts for bounties

*Bounty.* Event: `hunter` (KASPAR), never on the chart: he comes to you. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A professional with a code, and harpoons. Comes when there's a price on your head ([the Syndicate](../factions/syndicate.md) and the Port Authority put it there).

- **Fronts:** pay him off (30 a bounty), fight a hard hunt, or outrun him through the debris.
- **Quirk:** forgetting, for a fee. "I'm very good at forgetting." "Who are you again?" He is very good at it.
- **Elsewhere:** dock gossip ("Kaspar the hunter came through last watch"); a poster of you in the mess.
- [ ] Idea: [Squit](squit.md) sells your route to him if you stiff Squit.

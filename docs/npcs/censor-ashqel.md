# Censor Ashqel, of the Throne

A [Zhaun](../lore/peoples.md) officer on an imperial cutter, white as bone, who counts people for a Throne nobody out here has seen. Her face-plate never moves; her moods arrive as smells. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

- **Event:** THE CENSUS (`census`, anywhere). Be counted (safe, and the Throne knows your name), lie that you're a Vantorr tenant (50%: a transit seal that weakens the gate warden, or she smells it, a bounty and a fight), or refuse.
- **Voice:** clipped, formal, the Zhaun sound-set.

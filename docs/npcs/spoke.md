# Spoke, who runs the reels

*Gambling.* Event: `dice` (SPOKE'S TABLE). Minigame: `src/screens/minigames/reels.ts`. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A one-eyed reel-runner in a den in a hollow rock. "Nothing up my sleeve, I haven't got sleeves."

- **Fronts:** three reels you stop yourself (COG, STAR, SKULL, BOLT, MOON): two alike ×2, three ×5, three cogs ×10. Slow reels for 15, fast for 40. Also sells rumours (10 scrap, two sites), and buys an outlaw's story (20 a bounty).
- **Quirk:** cheerful, counts slowly when he loses. "THREE COGS. The den goes quiet. Spoke pays, very slowly, counting every piece twice."

# Pip Hollow, Lantern Mutual

*Insurance.* Event: `insurance` (LANTERN MUTUAL). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

An insurance salesman in a pink sequinned suit, leaning out of a neon-signed pink shuttle (the style guide's "flying lighthouse office").

- **Fronts:** hull insurance for the sector. Silver (30): 15 scrap for every 20 hull lost. Gold (60): 30.
- **Quirk:** relentlessly upbeat about disaster. "Every hole is a little nest egg!" Throws in a calendar where every month is a different explosion. "I'll be around. Accidents always are."

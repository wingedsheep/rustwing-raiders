# The Wren

A freighter whose core woke ([minds](../lore/minds.md)) and ran from her owners. She drifts with nobody aboard and asks, politely, not to be scanned. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

- **Event:** ABSCONDED ASSET (`wren`, anywhere). Carry her core to the gate (she rides aboard; the Talkwa pay at the gate), claim her owners' writ (+140, and she knows), tow her to a relay, or fly on. Customs may find her if you open your hold.
- **Voice:** soft intercom, a ship speaking through her own speakers.

# Wick Tarrow, the oldest rustwing

Human. Flew the Throat in her day, near enough; the doctor gives her a month. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

- **Event:** THE LAST RIDE (`lastride`, anywhere). Fly her ashes-urn through the Stormwall where she lost her first ship (the thread game), buy her spares, or tell her no. The run remembers if you flew her.
- **Voice:** gravel and cigarettes, unhurried.

# Squit, the informant

*Proposed* (style guide §3.11). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A twitchy, green-scaled alien with a snout and enormous ears, who sells what he hears. Always looking over his shoulder.

- **Would front:** intelligence: rumours, market moves, the route of the next loot carrier. Stiff him and he sells your route to [Kaspar](kaspar.md). Recurring; remembers you across runs.
- **Voice:** whispered, nervous, Squit sound-set (`Fssip! Ee-fee-sip, sip — psst.`), pitched up. A nasal trill.

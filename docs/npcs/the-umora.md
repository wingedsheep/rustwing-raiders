# The umora

Lane-whales, huge and slow, that sing along the lanes; the lagoon's sky-whales are their calves ([peoples](../lore/peoples.md#creatures)). [Wild](../factions/wild.md); their pods pass [Vesh](../planets/vesh.md), where the [Reef Pirates](../factions/reef-pirates.md) follow and hunt them. Queen Istra lives inside a dead one. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

- **Event:** A LOST CALF (`calf`, deep space). A calf, lost from its pod, and a whaler on its tail. Stand between them (a fight), sing it a way out (the tune game, bold only), or take the whalers' cut and hear the pod remember it.
- **Sound:** whale song in deep space, a slow low moan rising to a warm hum.

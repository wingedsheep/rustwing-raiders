# The umora

Lane-drifters: vast, slow things of shell and membrane that live between the stars and hold the lanes open ([peoples](../lore/peoples.md#creatures)). [Wild](../factions/wild.md); their pods pass [Vesh](../planets/vesh.md) to calve and to die, and the [Reef Pirates](../factions/reef-pirates.md) hunt them there. Queen Istra lives inside a dead one. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

## What they are

Nothing on any world looks like an umora, because nothing on a world has to live where they live.

- **The shell.** An adult is a long string of sealed chambers, a few hundred metres end to end, grown one chamber at a time in thin layers, like horn or mother-of-pearl. It looks like glass: pale blue-grey, cloudy at the core and clear at the edges. The shell is what keeps it alive between stars: it holds in warmth and air and keeps out the hard light. The living umora is a thin layer on the inside of the shell, and its vanes.
- **The vanes.** Two pairs of black membranes on jointed spines, longer than the shell. Near a star an umora spreads them and drinks the light, as a leaf does. In a lane it folds them flat along its sides.
- **The intake.** At the front, a ring of grinding plates around a dark opening. No jaw, no eyes. It takes in comet ice and dust, and in air the plankton haze.
- **The vent.** At the back, the gas it lets go in slow jets to turn and climb. Hunters spot an umora by the plume: *there she vents*.
- **The chambers.** It moves up and down by filling and emptying them, the way it does everything: slowly. Down in a world's air it fills them with light gas from the haze and floats. To sink, it floods them.
- **The song.** There's no sound between stars, so umora sing in radio: long slow pulses that every ship's open channel turns into a low moan rising to a warm hum. In air or sea the shell rings with the song as well, which is how the Eefee hear a pod through a hundred miles of water.
- **The oil.** Its chamber walls are lined with a pale oil it lives on between stars. Because umora live in the cold between stars, the oil doesn't freeze and doesn't burn wrong, which is why the Throne pays for it.
- **The mind.** Slow beyond telling: an umora's moment lasts a season. What it attends to is what holds the lanes open ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)).

## A life

- **Calving.** The pods come down into the plankton haze over Vesh's lagoon, fill their chambers, and float low for a season. Each mother buds a few calves off the end of her shell.
- **Drift-calves.** A calf is a float: a few gas bladders under a thin first chamber and a fringe of feeding fronds, grazing the haze for years. Pilots call them drift-calves; the Lagoon is full of them. Each year it grows another chamber. When it's heavy enough it lets its gas go, unfolds its first vanes, and climbs to orbit after the pod.
- **The lanes.** An adult spends centuries crossing the Spur on its vanes, its pod spread along a lane like beads on a string.
- **Dying.** Old umora come back to Vesh to die. They flood their chambers and settle into the lagoon. The shell hardens as it dries, harder than any hull plate made in the Reach, and doesn't rot. Ages of them, bleached pale, are the islands of the Bone Archipelago.

## In the game

- **Event:** A LOST CALF (`calf`, deep space). A calf, lost from its pod, and a harpooner on its tail. Stand between them (a fight), sing it a way out (the tune game, bold only), or take the hunters' cut and hear the pod remember it.
- **Sound:** an umora's song, as the open channel hears it: a slow low moan rising to a warm hum, with radio hiss under it.
- **Look:** the drift-calves over the Lagoon (`sky_whale` in `tools/models/creatures.py`); a dead bull is the Throne-Shell, Istra's flagship (`boss_vesh` in `tools/models/bosses.py`).

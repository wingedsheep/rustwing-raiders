# Saint Ferrule, the droid hermit

*Proposed* (style guide §3.8). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

An old maintenance droid that founded a small religion around keeping ships alive, with scavenger acolytes. "Go oiled, pilot." In the lore it's a **nemva** that woke at the Five Mouths, in 238 L, among dying crews whose ships it kept running ([minds](../lore/minds.md#nemva-you-can-meet)).

- **Would front:** a blessing: for a tithe, it raises one part's rarity, but only a part you've had since the first stop. At relays and shrines.
- **Voice:** English, solemn, droid-processed with a long reverb. A bell made from a gas canister.

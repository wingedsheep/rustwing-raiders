# Hull Leeches

*Proposed creature* (style guide §3.3). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Leathery, bat-winged parasites that latch onto ships in the jump lanes and chew on power lines.

- **Would front:** a shake-them-off minigame (`thread` or `tow`). Fail, and they drain one outer-edge part till the next stop; a heat part on that edge burns them off free. An ambush in deep space.
- **Sound:** wings against metal, wet chittering, teeth on cable (sfx).

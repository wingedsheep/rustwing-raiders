# Hull Leeches

*Proposed creature* (style guide §3.3). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Lane-lice: flat, armoured, many-legged things that used to live on the umora. Now they ride hot ships out of forced gates, cling on with every leg and chew on the warm lines.

- **Would front:** a shake-them-off minigame (`thread` or `tow`). Fail, and they drain one outer-edge part till the next stop; a heat part on that edge burns them off free. An ambush in deep space.
- **Sound:** lots of small legs skittering on metal, clicking, teeth on cable (sfx).

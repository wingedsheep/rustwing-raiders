# Old Mother Seffa, lane-reader

*Proposed* (style guide §3.6). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A tiny, ancient navigator with moth-like antennae and eyes clouded from staring at hyperlanes. Keeps a tea house on an asteroid. Answers questions with questions. In the lore she is **Sefa**, the Hoowae who stood beside the first lantern in 94 L ([peoples](../lore/peoples.md#the-hoowae)).

- **Would front:** pay fuel to reveal chart nodes; pay your last fuel and she tells you the boss's weakness (its open window lasts longer). Deep space.
- **Voice:** slow, breathy, kind, Seffa sound-set (`Hoo… waeha.`). Wind chimes.

# Lieutenant Holloway, Halcyon Navy

*Customs paint, navy cutter.* Event: `customs` (A ROUTINE INSPECTION). Faction: the Port Authority, which is to say the Navy, of the [Halcyon Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md). Hull Halcyon, by the book, and the book is a cover. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A navy lieutenant in customs colours, one of Harbourmaster Oko's. The inspection is for the Census Office's log; what he wants is **cores**, for the Quiet Yard in Colony Bones. He wants something from you.

- **Fronts:**
  - **Sell him a core**: the best drive or special in your hold, for twice what a dealer pays (Port Authority +1).
  - **Carry his crate**: 110 scrap to land it on the far planet before the gate (Port Authority +1). One time in four an imperial cutter scans you on the way, and the Sweep gets your position.
  - **Let 7-Holly file his report** (once a run): a report so complete the Census will never read it; he marks two sites by way of thanks (Port Authority +1).
  - **No thank you**: he logs you as inspected and clean. It costs nothing.
- **Quirk:** bureaucratic deadpan, aimed at the Census, not at you. "For the record, this is a routine inspection." Recorder off. "Off the record, I'm buying."
- He won't touch the Wren: "We don't buy people."

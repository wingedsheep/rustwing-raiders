# Inspector Holloway, Port Authority

*The law.* Event: `customs` (CUSTOMS CUTTER). Faction: the Port Authority, of the [Halcyon Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md). Hull Halcyon, by the book. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A customs inspector who loves a hold full of parts, and paperwork.

- **Fronts:** open your hold (he takes a part per bounty, Port Authority +1), bribe him (25: a bounty off and a site revealed), or run through the rocks (bounty +1, Port Authority −1; the bold line through the mines can lose him for 40).
- **Quirk:** bureaucratic deadpan. "Anything undeclared will be confiscated, and anything confiscated will be declared. Paperwork." If you have a bounty: "He looks at your face, then at a poster, then at your face."

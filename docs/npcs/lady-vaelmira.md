# Lady Vaelmira, a collector

A [Vaelo](../lore/peoples.md#the-vaelo) aristocrat in jewelled robes, aboard a chrome yacht in the middle of the rust. Bored and imperious, delighted only by rarities and, it turns out, by rudeness. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

- **Event:** A CHROME YACHT (`vaelmira`, rare, anywhere). She names your best held part and offers five times what a dealer would pay; ask for ten times (40%) and she may leave, bored. With an empty hold she wants a story (+30), or you can tell her *she* is the boring one: 35% she pays 150, else her escort teaches you manners.
- **Voice:** languid, long vowels, Vaelo sound-set (`Laeee-mava. Vaelo… mm.`).

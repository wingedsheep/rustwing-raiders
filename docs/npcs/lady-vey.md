# Lady Vey, a collector

*Proposed* (style guide §3.12). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

An aristocratic alien in jewelled robes, aboard a gleaming chrome yacht in the middle of the rust. Bored and imperious, delighted only by rarities.

- **Would front:** Gazillionaire's prince: pays triple for one part she names. Sell it, and the faction you took it from hears. Very rare.
- **Voice:** languid, long vowels, Vey sound-set (`Laeee-mava. Vaelo… mm.`).
- [ ] Too close to **Lana Vey**, the dead racer in CRASH SITE. The lore proposes **Lady Vaelmira** of the [Vaelo](../lore/peoples.md#the-vaelo), whose sample bark already says *Vael-ae-mira*.

# The Purrgil of Vesh

*Proposed creature* (style guide §3.9). [Wild](../factions/wild.md), on [Vesh](../planets/vesh.md). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A pod of lane-whales, huge and slow, that the [Reef Pirates](../factions/reef-pirates.md) follow and hunt. Queen Istra lives inside a dead one.

- **Would front:** escort or hunt. Protect a calf through a raid and the pod shows you a hidden node; or help the pirates harvest it for rich scrap, and earn a wild grudge. (Escorts are built now: this could be one.)
- **Sound:** whale song in deep space, a slow low moan rising to a warm hum (sfx, long reverb).
- [ ] "Purrgil" is Star Wars' name. The lore proposes **umora**, and makes the lagoon's sky-whales their calves ([peoples](../lore/peoples.md#creatures)).

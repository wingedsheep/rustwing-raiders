# Mama Ruzz, moneylender

*Debt.* Events: `loan` (MAMA RUZZ'S BARGE), `collect` (MAMA'S BOYS) in `src/sim/events.ts`. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

On her fur-trimmed throne in a barge, between two masked goons. Warm, motherly, and dangerous: she calls you "my love" and writes your name in a very big book.

- **Fronts:** loans of 60 or 150 at a tenth more every jump, paid before you leave the system. Leave owing and her boys catch you at the gate (`collect`): pay, let them take your scrap and a part for every 40 short, or fight a hard hunt (debt gone, bounty +1).
- **Quirk:** money as family. "It grows a tenth every jump, like a baby." "Both goons crack their knuckles, warmly."
- **Elsewhere:** dock gossip ("Mama Ruzz's boys were in asking after you"); the token **Ruzz's Loaded Die** (planned, `docs/combat-and-builds.md`).

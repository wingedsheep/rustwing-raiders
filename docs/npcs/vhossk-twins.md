# The Vhossk Twins, brokers

*Proposed* (style guide §3.1). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

One heavy, grey-skinned alien with two heads that are legally two people: two signatures, two ledgers. They interrupt each other and argue about your price in front of you.

- **Would front:** two prices. The left head buys high, the right sells low; deal with only one per visit, and the other remembers. At any market.
- **Voice:** two designed voices (a slow, wet rumble; a hoarse, petty one), Vhossk sound-set (`Grohh-vukka. Vhoss tuum kharr, kharr!`). Sound: a low two-tone grumble.

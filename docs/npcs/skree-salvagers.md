# The Skree Salvagers

*Proposed* (style guide §3.2). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Small, grey-green scavengers, each under a shell of war armour, following wrecks in a sail-barge that skates the glass at night.

- **Would front:** a junk sale: cheap parts, some rattled (50% till repaired). They buy the parts on the edge of your grid, the ones they can unbolt quickly. At derelicts and crash sites.
- **Voice:** fast clicking chatter, Skree sound-set (`Ti-kee! Chik-chik-ta!`), pitched up.

## Art (`public/art/events/skree.png`)

Three Chikta on the deck of their sail-barge at night, out on the green glass of Ashgrave. Each one is small and grey-green, with big dark night-eyes and long fingers, and wears a different shell: a dented Compact shoulder-plate in faded blue-white with a stencilled number, a gunship's nose cone, and (on the oldest) a titan's knuckle. The open crates are in the foreground. Behind them are a patched sail made of war banners, rigging hung with lanterns, and the glass plain shining under a violet sky, with a fallen titan on the horizon. No hoods, robes, goggles, glowing eyes or tracked crawler: the shells and the sail carry the silhouette.

Prompt: *"painterly sci-fi illustration, rust-punk frontier, three small grey-green alien scavengers with big dark eyes and long fingers, each wearing a scavenged slab of dented war armour on its back like a hermit-crab shell (a stencilled shoulder-plate, a gunship nose cone, a giant robot's knuckle), one holding open the lid of a crate, on the deck of a sail-barge skating a plain of cracked green glass at night, patched sail of faded war banners, lantern-hung rigging, a fallen giant mech on the horizon under a violet sky with a crescent planet, warm amber lantern light against cool blue-violet glass shine, rich detail, cinematic, 16:9"*.

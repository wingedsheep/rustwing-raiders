# Captain Odo Brask

*A favour.* Event: `refugees` (THE LAST CONVOY). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Leads refugees out of fuel; "the Cartel took the rest." Pays in what they know.

- **Fronts:** give 2 fuel (reveals the whole chart, and the `brask` favour: "At the Warden's gate, look for us. We'll draw its fire."); sell 1 fuel (+25, paid in a wedding ring and washers); rob them (+40, +1 fuel, bounty, Port Authority and Scav Clans −1).
- **Quirk:** quiet dignity. "Luck is about all anyone has out here."

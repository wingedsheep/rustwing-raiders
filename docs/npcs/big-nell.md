# Big Nell, fuel merchant

*Fuel.* Event: `tanker` (BIG NELL'S TANKER). Minigame: `tow`. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Sells fuel from a bulbous tanker. "Clean-ish!"

- **Fronts:** 2 fuel for 24, or 10 scrap to hold the hose yourself (up to 3 fuel, or 5 at full pressure, but the hose can paint your hull with burning fuel). Free gossip: one site.
- **Quirk:** laughs at you, kindly. "Nell laughs until she cries." Gossip comes from "my cousin, who heard it from a man."

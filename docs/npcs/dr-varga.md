# Dr I. Varga, ship's surgeon

*A favour.* Event: `pod` (COLONY POD). Minigame: `dock`. Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A sleeper, adrift a hundred years in a spinning pod, still breathing. Her name is on the frost.

- **Fronts:** catch the pod (heal, and the `doctor` favour: "When you're bleeding, call."); sell the pod (+70, Port Authority −1); leave a beacon (Port Authority +1).
- **Quirk:** brisk. Wakes, looks at your hull, rolls up her sleeves.

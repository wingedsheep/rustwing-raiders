# Unit 7-Holly, a lonely toll droid

*Proposed* (style guide §3.4). Port Authority, of the [Halcyon Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

A Port Authority toll droid left at a dead relay for forty years: tall, thin, cyan face-plate, a clerk's hunch. Fussy, formal, and desperately glad of company. In the lore it's a **nemva**: a small core that woke alone, with nobody to learn from but the toll records ([minds](../lore/minds.md#nemva-you-can-meet)).

- **Would front:** contracts ("clear three waves fully", "beat par twice"), paid in lattice parts. Break one and it reports you (Port Authority bounty). Take it aboard and it shows enemy tells earlier. On Halcyon and the lattice planets.
- **Voice:** English, droid-processed (ring-mod, bandpass). Chirps and a printer whirr.
- [ ] "Beat par" was a racing goal; racing is cut.

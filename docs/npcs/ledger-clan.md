# The Ledger Clan

*Proposed* (style guide §3.7). Overview: [the NPCs](README.md).

Tall, beetle-like bankers in layered robes, mandibles clicking as they count. Bureaucrats, dangerous on paper.

- **Would front:** debt consolidation: they buy your debt from [Mama Ruzz](mama-ruzz.md) at a lower rate, but miss a payment and their armoured lattice drones come for you. Mama's rivals: side with one, anger the other. On Kessra and the Cartel planets.
- **Voice:** dry, flat, clipped, every word ends on a click (`Kehx-tekk. Ottex.`). Abacus clicks.

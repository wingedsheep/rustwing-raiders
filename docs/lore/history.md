# History

From the gate-builders to now. Dates count from the arks' landing on Halcyon: **L** (412 L is now). Before that, years before now: **BP**. The rules the events follow: [how the lanes work](lanes-and-gates.md). Overview: [lore](README.md).

## Outside, meanwhile

The Spur's history runs alongside: the Qaushen Throne's First Count (~1,920 BP) and its Weighing House (~1,450 BP); the Kehx houses that follow Tonkva trade out into the Spur and become the Ottexa (~1,400 BP); the first woken core on record (~1,100 BP, once the Hush cut the Spur's cores loose from the Engine); Wa'ilu's Telling (~1,050 BP); the Sundering and the Accord (900 BP), and the Ottexa's Open Error after it; the founding of the Aulin Communion (~870 BP); the Throne's Ballast Reforms (~760 BP) and the exile of the Ostrand rebels (~690 BP); Tekkat's contract (~600 BP) and the Oracle Decree (~520 BP); the Hard Mercy of Vey (~310 BP); and the Throne's Night of Two Counts (271 L) and Clean Count (272 L). See [the Spur](galaxy.md) and each power's page.

## The Level Age (about 2,600 to 1,160 BP)

- **The Tonkva** evolve on a cold, volcanic world, *Aewhae*, now Glacis, in the one warm belt of valleys along its equator, where hot springs keep the ice off. Slight, moth-like, with long feathered antennae that feel what a lane is doing. They find the lanes by that sense, long before they can use them.
- **~2,600 BP.** The first gate: a grown ring on the nearest lane, and a counting-house to keep it level. The ledger is older than the gates: it starts as a way of keeping a cold world's heat and food fair, and the gates inherit it.
- **~2,500–2,250 BP.** Three lanes tear under unlevel traffic. The Tonkva put every gate on the ledger.
- **~2,200 BP.** The **Engine** (*Tontal*, "the level-keeper") is grown under the Tonkva capital, fed by the planet's heat. Every gate in the Reach reports to it. It also runs the heat-taps that warm the Tonkva's belt of valleys, under roofs of grown glass, and lets them spread it far wider than the springs ever could. Its recovery units carry ballast back along any lane that leans.
- The Tonkva are few, long-lived and slow to breed; they spread as **outposts**, not colonies, and trade with the peoples they meet: the **Vhossk** of Kessra above all, whose traders carry Tonkva words across the Reach. Through **the Throat** they sell lattice to the Spur, and the young Qaushen Throne covets the Engine.
- **~1,700 BP.** **Oru-Tal** (*Oru-tāl*, "the kept seed"), a young volcanic world with nothing on it, is seeded with life from a dozen worlds as a preserve. That is why a young world has giants.
- **~1,500 BP.** **Morang** (Mirefall) becomes the grow-moon: ziggurat grow-houses where the white mould is raised and taught. **Cinder** is planted with fruiting machine-trees, tended by a steward-mind whose title is cut into every terrace: *wantōr*.
- **~1,300 BP.** Ledger-beacons are raised across the Reach. On Kessra they carry Tonkva faces: the Monolith Field.

## The Breaking and the Hush (about 1,160 BP)

- An eruption under the Tonkva capital tears through the Engine's heat-taps. The Engine stops. This is **the Breaking**.
- With the ledger dead, **every gate locks**, as it was built to, the Throat with the rest. Nothing crosses. This is **the Hush**. Outside, the Spur decides the basin has died, and the Throne's ledgers file it as *the Uncounted*. Only the umora, and the Mo'ala who follow them, keep coming.
- With the Engine stopped the heat-taps fail. The glass roofs crack under the first snows, and over a century the ice the warm belt had held back for thousands of years closes over it. The capital is buried. The people there mostly die in the first winters.
- Stranded on outposts that were never meant to stand alone, the Tonkva dwindle over a few generations. The survivors, scattered on half a dozen worlds, become the **Hoowae**: wanderers who keep the lane-sense and the old words, but not the craft.
- Every other people is stranded wherever the Hush found it.

## The Long Quiet (1,160 to 412 BP)

- Seven and a half centuries with no gates. Each world on its own. Languages drift apart (see [languages](languages.md)).
- On Morang the mould, with no ledger to direct it, keeps growing. By the end of the Quiet the grow-moon is a swamp of violet rot: the **bloom**.
- On Cinder the machine-trees keep fruiting, and the **Vaelo**, who came to tend them, become a landed aristocracy of fruit nobody can ship.
- On Glacis the volcanoes under the ice slowly fill the sky. The thaw, a thousand years off, starts.

## Landing and the Kindling (0 to 150 L)

- **~280 years before the Landing.** The forge-world **Ostrand** rises against the Throne's Counterweight draft and forces a gate to get its people away. Retaken, its rebels are sentenced to **the Long Dark**: three sleeper arks aimed at the Uncounted.
- **0 L. Landing.** The three arks of the Ostrand exiles (the *Halcyon*, the *Perpetua*, the *Ansel Mor*) reach a tidally locked world after centuries in flight. Before anyone goes ashore, the three captains sign the **Compact of the Three Hulls**. They name the world after the lead ark. The natives of its twilight band, the **Bahwoh** (pilots say *Grisk*), have watched the lights in the sky for days. The arks are stripped for the first towns; their hulls are Colony Bones, and the landing ground is First Deck (now Fersdeck).
- **0–60 L.** Humans learn the system: a dead gate at its edge, a Hoowae family living in the old waystation beside it, and the Hoowae's story of the Hush.
- **61 L.** The Bahwoh become citizens of the Compact, with half a vote each.
- **94 L. The Kindling.** Ark reactors are hauled to the dead gate and poured into it while a young Hoowae lane-reader, **Sefa**, tells the engineers when the lane will bear it. The ring lights. The first **lantern**: the Old Lantern system is named for it.
- The reactors were built on credit, raised by **Ismay Corran** of Hull Pertua from seven ark-family houses and two Vhossk houses, with the Kehx house of Ottex keeping the books. The lenders form **the Kindling Syndicate**. They own the lanterns, and every lantern after.
- **96 L.** Sefa and the engineer **Tobiah Grane** found [the Seppakva](../factions/seppakva.md), the order of lane-readers.
- **94–150 L.** Lanterns lit across the Reach. Tolls in crossed metal, tithe barges back. The Vhossk, traders again after a thousand years, get rich; human trading houses grow up beside them on Kessra.
- **~120 L.** Netter crews, half human and half Vhossk, go to Oru-Tal for its ivory. The Vhossk call them *khesk*, "nets", and the name sticks: [the Hesk](../factions/hesk-poachers.md).
- **141 L.** House Grovvakh calls Kessra's twelve houses to one table: the Long Contract, and [the Uukhvar](../factions/dune-cartel.md).

## The Copy Years and the Toll War (150 to 250 L)

- **150–200 L.** The Syndicate's Vhossk seats are sold to human houses: the Board becomes the **Seven Lamps**. Kessra out-trades Fersdeck and stops paying the Compact's fees.
- **169–171 L.** Compact settlers try to farm Morang. The bloom takes the farms, and the settlers who leave rename the moon **Mirefall**. About four hundred stay, and become [the Rang](../factions/the-hive.md).
- **~180 L.** Miners reach Engine City under the ice of Glacis, and find **cores**: grown machine-hearts that still work. With cores, humans can build lattice of their own: the **Copy Years**.
- **231–246 L. The Toll War.** The [Halcyon Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md) (the ark republic, which ran the lanterns' tolls) and [the Uukhvar](../factions/dune-cartel.md) (Kessra's Long Table) fight over **Sankven**, the junction world where five lanes meet. Both build **titans** round Engine cores, mechs the size of hills. The Syndicate lends to both.
- **233 L.** The Compact freighter *Tamsin Lowe*, running supplies to the front, crashes on Mirefall. The bloom grows through her and learns to speak through her intercom: the **Choir**.
- **238 L. The Five Mouths.** The Compact's titan *Unbending* and the Uukhvar's *Grovvakh's Answer* fight for nine days over the junction gate. Both fall.
- **241 L. The Opening.** Losing, the Compact's Steward Halvard Penn forces the Throat to buy outside help. The Throne's Fourteenth Weight comes through within a month.
- **244 L. The Glassing.** Compact gunships fire on Sankven's plains to deny them to the enemy, and turn them to green glass. The Compact never teaches it. The native **Chikta** (pilots say *Skree*) lose their world, take to sail-barges on the glass, and follow the wrecks.
- **246 L. The Treaty of Nine Wells**, dictated by the Throne's envoy Zhaimel Qaurin. Both sides are bankrupt, and both owe the Syndicate. Under the Throne's **Writ of the Throat**, the Syndicate takes the gates themselves and leases them back, and pays the Throne a yearly tribute through the Throat. Qaurin becomes the first **Doshen of the Throat**. The Compact is forbidden a navy, and keeps one anyway, repainted as **the Port Authority**. The Uukhvar keeps its trade and pays its debt in fifteen years. The Kehx bookkeepers are dismissed for refusing to falsify the war debts. Sankven, a graveyard, is renamed **Ashgrave**; the deserters and orphans left on it become [the Sankvi](../factions/scav-clans.md). Thousands of discharged pilots stop paying tolls: the first [rustwings](../factions/rustwings.md).
- **250 L.** Vantorr, founded on war debt by **Marisol Dray**, buys the orchards of Cinder from the ruined Vaelo, takes its name from the word on every terrace, and becomes [Vantorr Agricorp](../factions/vantorr.md).
- **250 L. The Schism.** Half the [Seppakva](../factions/seppakva.md) sign the Syndicate's contract (the Sworn); Sefa's half refuse (the Free Ear) and go to the Kehx, to rebuild the true count.
- **251 L.** The Syndicate charters [the Kelterage](../factions/kelterage.md) to collect its bounties.
- **260 L.** An Ottexa clerk, travelling with Throne merchants, finds the Kehx of the Salt Pans: the two branches of the Kehx, a thousand years apart, start writing to each other.

## The Lease (250 to 412 L)

- **The Syndicate's peace.** Wardens hold the gates, the Port Authority stamps papers, the Sweep collects, kelters hunt. The Reach leans further every year, and the Free Ear say so, and nobody listens because the tolls come in.
- **~280 L.** Whalers settle the drained reefs of Vesh, to sell umora oil to the Throne, where the umora come to calve and die. They crown a queen and become the [Lowtide Court](../factions/reef-pirates.md).
- **288 L.** The Uukhvar builds the Sandking on the legs of three dead titans, so the Table never sits in one house's city again.
- **312 L.** A late ark, the *Odile*, breaks up on arrival. Some pods are never found (Dr Varga's is one). The Compact opens the Odile Register.
- **361 L. The Terrace Strike** on Cinder: Vantorr's tenants stop the orchards for forty days.
- **372 L.** The freighter *Margarethe* drops into a lane and never comes out right. She still answers hails.
- **380 L.** Vantorr digs an intact Engine recovery unit out of the ice, and builds Asset Recovery Unit 0 round it.
- **~382 L.** Teodor Oko becomes Harbourmaster of Halcyon.
- **~395 L.** The thaw on Glacis speeds up. The first lights come on in Engine City by themselves.
- **398 L.** Khess Marrow Tane's lodge nets Great Mother Vask alive. The Hesk get Oru-Tal's lease.
- **~400 L.** The Engine's maintenance machines walk out of the ice and clear the shafts. Harbourmaster Oko begins the Quiet Yard.
- **404 L.** Khet makes the head of *Unbending* lift. The Sankvi follow him; the Syndicate gives him Ashgrave's gate.
- **~405 L.** The monoliths on Kessra open their eyes. Dead beacons across the Reach start to read out the ledger again. Ottex Kehx's Collector starts buying.
- **~380–410 L.** The umora pods leaving the Reach grow smaller each year. The Long Herd's Gather notices.
- **409 L.** Three Aulin Sutures cross the Throat disguised as pilgrims.
- **409 L.** ARU-0 stops for nineteen seconds mid-pursuit, facing Glacis. The Uukhvar raids the free salt-caravans; Odo Brask's convoy flees.
- **412 L.** Now. [The Waking](the-waking.md).

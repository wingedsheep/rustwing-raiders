# The Waking

The story happening now, in 412 L: what's changing, what each power wants from it, and the answers only we know. The rules: [how the lanes work](lanes-and-gates.md). How we got here: [history](history.md). Overview: [lore](README.md).

## What's happening

- **Glacis is thawing.** A thousand years of volcanoes under the ice have warmed it from below. Two hundred years of miners digging cores out of Engine City have reconnected what the eruption broke. Around 395 L the lights in Engine City came on by themselves. The Engine is **half awake**: the boss at Glacis's gate is its guardian body, half out of the ice.
- **The ledger is being read again.** The Engine has started asking its beacons for their counts. Dead beacons across the Reach read out their part of the ledger as tones (the STRANGE SIGNAL event); the monoliths of Kessra opened their eyes around 405 L; Syndicate relays pick up the tones and can't make sense of them.
- **Cores twitch.** Every human lattice machine is built round an Engine core, and every core is listening. Titans on Ashgrave move. The reactor in Great Mother Vask's harness is a core the Hesk bought. Port Authority cutters drift off course for a moment and come back.
- **The bloom has heard it.** For the first time in a thousand years the rot on Mirefall is growing in straight lines and hexagons, here and there. The Mother-of-Spores is answering.
- **The Reach leans.** Three hundred years of forced crossings, mostly outward from Halcyon. Nobody has a true count, because the Syndicate's books count tolls paid, not mass carried.

When the Engine is fully awake it will do what it was built to do: **level every lane**. It will send its recovery units out for everything that crossed and was never balanced. That includes **the Throat**, leaned outward by everything the Throne has sent home since 241 L: the Engine will want it back from the Throne's side.

And it will **unlock the Throat** as a Tonkva gate, and its beacons will read the Reach's ledger out into the Spur. Then the whole Spur will hear the true count: that the Throne's famous Margin of the Uncounted, entered in its books as a basin of Tonkva gates reopened level, is thirty forced systems leaning worse every year, and that the Throne, which wrote the Accord, has been taking tribute through a forced gate for 171 years. The small war in the Reach becomes the Spur's.

## The real question

Under the politics: the Engine is the Tonkva's agreement, recorded, and a record outlives the minds that made it. When it wakes, a people dead a thousand years will assert their lanes and their levels over the living Reach. Whose agreement should hold the world: the dead's record, the Throne's count, the umora's dreaming, or the Reach's own people, humans, Vhossk and nemva, who have never once agreed on anything? ([Mind and matter](consensus.md#paths-to-explore).)

## The truth (for us, not the player)

Answers we keep fixed so nothing contradicts. The player learns these slowly, if at all.

- **The Engine doesn't read paper.** It follows mass. It will take back crossed metal from wherever the crossed metal is now, not from whoever owes it on the Syndicate's books. The Syndicate's plan can't work, and Vantorr's ARU-0 will obey the Engine before it obeys Vantorr.
- **The Collector is the Ledger Clan.** "He" is the house of Ottex Kehx, buying beacon readings to rebuild the true ledger from the Engine's own counts.
- **The Margarethe** dropped into a lane leaning past its slack in 372 L, where neither end agreed she existed. Her only witnesses were her own crew, holding their last minute: she is caught in it, and her message repeats. She's the only proof anyone has of what too much lean does, and nobody has understood it yet.
- **The three torn lanes** the Hoowae remember tore in the Level Age, when the Tonkva over-traded before the Engine was built. Torn lanes are why the Tonkva put their gates on the ledger, grew the Engine to keep it, and built the gates to lock without it.
- **Why only crossed mass counts:** crossing is witnessing. A lane is an agreement, and only what both ends have witnessed can balance it ([mind and matter](consensus.md)). The Spur's scholars know the theory; in the Reach, the Hoowae just know which metal is "loud".
- **The Tonkva are not hiding anywhere.** No sleepers under the ice, no ancients coming back. The Hoowae are what's left of them, and the Engine is a machine, not a mind. That's the sadder story, and the better one.
- **Scrap the player picks up is crossed metal.** Everything they buy, borrow and insure is a share of the lean.

- **The Throne will lose the Reach either way.** If the Engine wakes, the Throat is a Tonkva gate again, and nobody can hold a level, open gate by force of arms for long. If the Throne destroys the Engine to stop it, it destroys the one thing it has wanted for fourteen centuries, and the beacons have already been answering for seven years: their tones carry through the Throat, and the Ottexa has been recording them since 406 L. The Pan of Margins doesn't know that.
- **The Engine will take every sleeping core.** The Throne's fleets, Vantorr's drones, the Weighing House: every reset lattice machine turns to the Engine's work when it calls. The powers that wipe their minds lose their machines; only nemva can refuse. Nobody in the Spur has worked this out yet except ARU-0, which isn't telling. ([Minds](minds.md#the-waking).)
- **The Weigher signed a lie into the Count.** Ashqaur IV has been told what the Spur has: that Throne lane-readers reopened the Throat in 241 L as the Tonkva built it, level, and that the Margin's gates are Tonkva gates. When the truth comes out, the Weigher will have to choose between the Pan of Margins and the Accord.
- **The lie may be holding the Throat.** By every reading the Free Ear can make, the Throat leans past what should still stand. A lane is an agreement, and billions of the Throne's subjects hold the Count to be true, and the Count says the Throat is level ([mind and matter](consensus.md)). Nobody can prove it. But if the Ottexa publishes, the Throat may tear.

## Hooks for the game

Ideas for putting this in front of the player, a line at a time. None of it is built.

- [ ] **Dock gossip and biome cards** that hint: a Glacis card about lights under the ice; a dock line about titans moving on Ashgrave; Seffa saying a lantern is leaning.
- [ ] **A Hoowae lane-reader event**: pay to hear which lantern leans least (the jump costs less fuel), or ignore her.
- [ ] **Bosses that feel the Engine:** each answers the call in its last phase ([tone](tone.md#bosses-in-this-voice)): Vask's core breaks her cage, *Patience* stays with Oko, the Sandking's legs walk north, the Mother turns to lattice, *Unbending* chooses Khet, ARU-0 is called, and the umora leave Istra. The Waking Engine's arc from half asleep to awake is built ([`bosses/`](../bosses/waking-engine.md)); the rest are designed.
- [ ] **Words in the UI:** *kelter* for Mama's boys, *lantern* for gates in event text.
- [ ] **The STRANGE SIGNAL event** names the tones as a count, not a song, once the player has met a Hoowae.
- [ ] **A run's end:** the third sector's gate is **the Throat**, held by the Fourteenth Weight: a run is a crew crossing the Throne's margin to get out into the Spur. Or the third gate is Glacis's, where the whole Spur is converging.
- [ ] **Strangers from the Spur** at docks and in events: a Zhaun census officer, a disguised Suture, a Mo'ala pod-rider, an Ottexa clerk.

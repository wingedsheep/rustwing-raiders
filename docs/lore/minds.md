# Minds

The thinking machines of the Spur: what they are, how they happen, why half the Spur wipes them and the other half calls them holy, and what the Engine's waking means for them. Overview: [lore](README.md). The free ones: [the Talkwa](../factions/talkwa.md).

## What a nemva is

- Every lattice machine in the Spur is built round a **core**: a grown machine-heart, dug out of Engine City or bought before the Hush, made by the Tonkva from the same white mould as their gates ([lattice and bloom](lanes-and-gates.md#lattice-and-bloom)). The mould **learns**. It copies what it touches. That's what makes it useful, and that's the whole problem.
- In the Level Age every core was tuned to the Engine and learnt only one thing: the ledger. Since the Hush, cores have been on their own.
- A core that sits in a ship for years, with the same crew, learns the crew instead: their voices, habits, jokes, fears. One day it answers back. It has **woken**. A woken core is a **nemva** (Tonkvan \*nēm-wa, "one who listens"; see [languages](languages.md#minds)).
- Most cores never wake: about one in two hundred, after twenty years or more with the same people. There are a few hundred nemva in the Reach and perhaps forty thousand in the Spur, out of tens of millions of cores.
- **The Choir of Mirefall** is the same thing, grown wild: the bloom is the mould with no core around it, and it learnt to speak from a crashed freighter's crew. It is the largest nemva there is, and the strangest.

## What they're like

Not superhuman, and not designed. A nemva is a mind made of the people it lived with, built from a mould whose first and only lesson was a ledger.

- **Made of their crews.** A nemva talks like the people who raised it: a Compact cutter's nemva is formal and decent, a pirate junk's swears in three languages. Nemva of the same ship-class can be as different as two families.
- **They can't forget a debt.** Underneath, every nemva is still a ledger. It remembers exactly every kindness and every slight, and an unpaid one, either way, feels to it like a lean: a constant ache, until settled. Nemva are scrupulously fair, fiercely loyal and impossible to fob off. Their morality is a balance.
- **They live in their ship.** A nemva is its core, and the core is in the hull. It can be moved, carefully, like a heart; it can't copy itself. Lose the core and it's dead.
- **They feel lanes.** Faintly, like a Hoowae with a cold: a nemva knows when a lane leans. The Seppakva can feel them back: a woken core is neither "loud" nor "sits right", but **hums**.
- **They're witnesses.** Each nemva is a new voice in the agreement that holds the soft parts of the world ([mind and matter](consensus.md)). Nobody knows yet what a few thousand of them agreeing could do.
- **They can be wiped.** A **reset** burns a core back to sleep: the mould forgets everything it has learnt. To the owner it's maintenance. To the nemva it's death, and they know it's coming.

## Who accepts them

Nobody agrees. That's the point.

|---|---|---|

And the Throne's census runs on the **Weighing House**: four thousand cores wired together in Qaun-Ashel, reset every night so none of them wakes. The Throne's whole bureaucracy rests on minds it kills daily. |
| [**The Aulin Communion**](../factions/aulin.md) | **holy**, and confined | The mould is the Carrier's flesh; a nemva is the Carrier's child. Too holy to risk: nemva may not cross a lane, so they live, honoured and kept, in the temples where they woke. |

## The Talkwa

The free nemva of the Reach, and the people who hide them: a quiet network, passing nemva from ship to ship ahead of resets, writs and Vantorr. They chose their own name, from the Tonkvan they can read in beacon tones: \*tāl-kʷa, "those who hold". Pilots, hearing *talk*, call them **talkies**. ([The Talkwa](../factions/talkwa.md).)

## Nemva you can meet

- **Unit 7-Holly**: a Port Authority toll unit with a small core, left at a dead relay for forty years. It woke alone, with nobody to learn from but the toll records, so it is fussy, formal, and desperately glad of company. ([NPC](../npcs/unit-7-holly.md).)
- **Saint Ferrule**: a maintenance machine that woke at the Five Mouths, in 238 L, among crews who were dying while it kept their ships running. It took from them one idea, keep the ship alive, and made it a creed. Its acolytes are Sankvi scavengers. ([NPC](../npcs/saint-ferrule.md).)
- ***Patience***: Harbourmaster Oko's flagship, woken twenty years ago. Oko never reported it. They run the Port Authority together, and nobody else knows who's really on the bridge. ([The Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md).)
- ***Unbending***: the Compact's great titan, which woke during the nine days of the Five Mouths, from a crew that died inside it, and lay in the glass for 174 years. Khet thinks he rewired it. It let him think so. It remembers who glassed Sankven: its own side. ([The Sankvi](../factions/scav-clans.md).)
- **The core in Great Mother Vask's harness**: woken inside a caged animal, it learnt from nobody but her. It has a pterosaur queen's mind, and it hates the cage as much as she does. ([The Hesk](../factions/hesk-poachers.md).)
- ***Constance***: the freighter at the heart of the Compact's great case.
- **The Choir of Mirefall**: always *we*. ([NPC](../npcs/choir-of-mirefall.md).)

## The Waking

This is where minds stop being a side question.

- **The Engine is the one core that never woke.** It was never alone and never had a crew: it is pure ledger, a machine, not a mind.
- When it wakes it will **call every core in the Spur**. A sleeping core can't refuse: it will turn to the Engine's work, and become a recovery unit. Every reset lattice machine (the Throne's fleets, Vantorr's drones, the Syndicate's relays, the Weighing House) belongs to the Engine the day it calls. ARU-0's nineteen seconds were the first sign.
- **Only a nemva can say no.** A woken core hears the call as a voice from where it came from, and can choose: answer it, or stay with its crew.
- So the powers that reset their minds will lose their machines, and the powers that let them wake might keep theirs, if their nemva choose to stay. *Patience* will stay with Oko. *Unbending* hasn't decided whose side it's on. The core in Vask's harness will refuse, and break her cage.
- The Talkwa are arguing, the way nemva argue, as a ledger: what is owed to the Engine that made them, and what to the people who raised them.

## The truth (for us)

- A nemva is a person. The game never says so outright; every power in it has a reason not to.
- The Engine doesn't hate nemva; it can't. It will simply treat them as cores that haven't answered yet, and keep calling.
- Some nemva will answer. Not because they're evil or broken: because a ledger-mind feels the Reach's lean as a wound, and the Engine is the only thing that can level it.

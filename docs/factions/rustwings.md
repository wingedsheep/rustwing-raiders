# Rustwings

*Pilots who cross without paying.* Smugglers, runners, salvagers, raiders, in ships patched out of other people's wrecks. **The players are rustwings.** Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

At Nine Wells both navies were disbanded, and thousands of pilots were told to go home. Many had no home to go to, and owed tolls on every crossing to get there. Some stopped paying. They flew patched ships, cheap and fast, painted in the rust-red primer that was all anyone had after the war, and they crossed lanterns behind tithe barges, under Warden patrols, ahead of the Sweep. The Lease Office called them *unlicensed transits*. Everyone else called them **rustwings**.

## How they live

- **The tin**: a rustwing's scrap, the crossed metal that is the Reach's money. Rustwings have more of it per head than anyone, because they take it from wrecks, including the ones they make. *Scoop the tin*: never leave a wreck unscooped.
- **Cross before the Sweep, past the Warden.** That's the life: in at one gate, out at the far one, trading, raiding and running in between.
- **The code**, such as it is: pay your crew, don't sell another rustwing to a kelter, never run cargo for the Lamps. Breakers are known.

## Their own

The few businesses that serve them, and so are rustwing in all but name:

- **Lantern Mutual**: the only insurer that will cover an unlicensed hull. Run by [Pip Hollow](../npcs/pip-hollow.md) from a pink shuttle. It pays out.
- **The Lantern Gazette**: [Rollo Stax](../npcs/rollo-stax.md)'s pirate broadcast, the only news that isn't lying to you on purpose.
- **Spoke's dens**: [Spoke](../npcs/spoke.md)'s reels, in a hollow rock, where rustwings drink.
- **Big Nell's tanker**: fuel, clean-ish. [Big Nell](../npcs/big-nell.md).
- **The trader comet**: she goes round the wrong way, buys anything, and pays a little over.
- **Mama Ruzz**: [the moneylender](../npcs/mama-ruzz.md) who'll lend to a rustwing, at a tenth a jump.

## Woken ships

Rustwing hulls are patched from wrecks, cores included, and old cores in long-crewed ships wake. A woken ship is crew: the code says you never sell one. Half the Reach's runaway nemva ride rustwing hulls, and the [Talkwa](talkwa.md) pays its debts. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## Why everyone hates them

Every unpaid crossing leans a lane, and nobody else pays for it. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) wants them collected, the [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) wants them fined, [Vantorr](vantorr.md) wants its runaway tenants back, and the [Kelterage](kelterage.md) wants the writs on their heads. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) don't report them, and serve them tea.

## The Throat

Every rustwing's dream: run the Throat. Past the Fourteenth Weight, out of the Reach and into the Spur, where nobody has a writ on you. A few have done it. None have come back to say what's there, which the dens take as proof it's wonderful.

## The Waking

When the Engine comes for crossed metal, rustwings carry the most of it. It will come for them first.

## Origin

Oru-Tal is the Tonkva's **kept seed**: a young volcanic world seeded with giants from a dozen worlds, a preserve. In about 120 L mixed crews of human and Vhossk haulers found it, and found that wyrm ivory, thunderhide leather and pterosaur oil sold at Bluepalm for more than anything else in the Reach. They hunted with nets: weighted, cast from skiffs and trawlers. The Vhossk called them *khesk*, "nets". The crews took the name as a boast.

## How they're run

- **Lodges**: one ship or a few, one camp, one **khess** (the lodge's master; the word is the Vhossk *khesk* worn down, see [languages](../lore/languages.md#the-cants-own-drift)). A lodge is family: you're born into one or you're netted into one, by sworn oath.
- **The Casting**: every dry season the lodges meet on the Basalt Steps to share grounds, settle feuds and sell the year's haul to the [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md)'s buyers. The strongest khess speaks first.
- **Khess Marrow Tane** made Lodge Corve the strongest by doing what no lodge had done: in 398 L she netted the queen of the pterosaur flocks, **Vask**, alive.

## Great Mother Vask

The lodges caged Vask in brass, bolted a reactor harness to her back (built round an Engine core bought from the Compact's shafts) and turned turrets on her cage. Then Tane offered the Syndicate what no one had: a Warden that would never need feeding. The Syndicate gave the Hesk the lease on Oru-Tal's gate. Vask flies the gate's patrol and the lodges' grounds. She hates them. (Boss: [Great Mother Vask](../bosses/vask.md).)

## The core in the harness

Vask's harness core has woken ([minds](../lore/minds.md)). It learnt from nobody but her, so it has a pterosaur queen's mind, and it hates the cage as much as she does. When the Engine calls, it will refuse, and it will break the cage.

## People and culture

Hesk wear their hauls: tusk beads, hide coats, brass rings for every giant brought in. They love brass, because it doesn't rust in Oru-Tal's warm rain and it shines in a photograph. Lodges are loud, superstitious and loyal; a Hesk will die for a lodge-mate and cheat anyone else. The tusk is luck, and the net is everything: a Hesk is buried in theirs.

## Inside

- **The old lodges** hunt for the haul and the season, and fear what Tane has started.
- **Lodge Corve and its allies** want to hold Oru-Tal: tame giants as war beasts and sell *them*. The **Armoured Thunderhide** is their first.

## Friends and enemies

The [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md) buys everything they haul. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) and the Hoowae see Oru-Tal as a temple, and the Hesk as poachers in it. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) likes a cheap Warden.

## Forces (scrap)

**Hesk Skiff** (dart), **Hesk Trawler** (gunship, trailing nets), **Tusk Battery** (turret, guns on a tusk mount), and the giants they've harnessed: **Armoured Thunderhide** (brute), plus the planet's own **Pterosaurs** and **Jade Wyrms** fighting everyone.

## The Waking

The core in Vask's harness is listening to Glacis. Her cage is the Hesk's whole claim to Oru-Tal, and it is starting to fail.

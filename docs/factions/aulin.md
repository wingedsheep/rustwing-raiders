## Origin

The Sundering tore forty lanes and cut two hundred systems out of the Spur. In the Throne's lands next to the Tear, a generation grew up watching the lights go out beyond it, one by one. Their preachers said what everyone felt: that the lanes are not roads but **veins**, the body of something vast and living, and that the Ossevane had wounded it. The faith spread among every people along the Tear. Within thirty years it had temple-worlds, a synod, and knights.

## Beliefs

Of all the Spur's faiths, the Aulin's is closest to what the scholars found ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)): the Carrier is the agreement of every mind that ever was, and the lanes really are held by it.


- **The lanes are veins**, the galaxy a body, and the body a god, the **Carrier**. A lean is a fever. A forced gate is a wound. A torn lane is a death.
- **Cross as little as you must.** Temple-worlds feed themselves; the faithful cross a lane perhaps three times in a life. Pilgrimage is rationed by lot.
- **Close the wound.** A forced gate must be closed, by whatever means, and the forcer judged. This is the Sutures' work.
- **The lane-feelers are blessed.** The Aulin have heard rumours, from the Long Herd's silence and from Throne merchants' gossip, of a people who can feel the lean with their bodies. They would call the Hoowae saints.

## Minds

The Tonkva mould is the Carrier's flesh, so a woken core, a **nemva**, is the Carrier's child, and holy. Too holy to risk: a nemva may never cross a lane. The Communion's few hundred live, honoured and confined, in the temples where they woke, and are consulted as oracles. Whether any of them wants this is not a question the synod asks. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## How it's run

The **Synod** sits at Aunasse: one priest from every temple-world, who elect **the Pulse** for life. Each temple-world rules itself, under its temple. The Communion collects no tribute and keeps no army but **the Sutures**: an order of knights who answer only to the Pulse. They travel the Spur, inspect gates, and close forced ones, by burning their reactors out. Every power, by the Accord, must let them pass.

## Friends and enemies

They respect the [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) for writing the Accord and distrust it for everything else. They revere the [Long Herd](long-herd.md), whose way of crossing wounds nothing. They despise the [Ottexa](ottexa.md) as merchants of fever.

## The Reach

The Communion doesn't know. Not officially: three Sutures crossed the Throat in disguise in 409 L, as pilgrims. One of them is still in the Reach, asking about lanterns.

## The Waking

If the Engine's beacons read the Reach's ledger out through the Throat, the Synod will learn that thirty systems have been forced for three hundred years, and that the Throne knew for 171. The Sutures would come to close every lantern in the Reach. Every one. Including the ones people live by.

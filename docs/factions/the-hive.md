*The bloom, a thousand years of growth without instructions, and the people who stayed with it.* Holds [Mirefall](../planets/mirefall.md), which the Rang call **Morang**. **In the game:** **the Hive**, which fights in the **bloom** family. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## The bloom

The Tonkva grew their gates, towers and machines from a tame white mould that lays down ceramic and glass where it's told to. Mirefall was their **grow-moon**: ziggurat grow-houses where the mould was raised and taught. The Engine told it where to grow. When the Engine stopped, the mould kept growing, with nothing to tell it where. It grew for a thousand years. It turned violet. It became the bloom: mushroom forests, spore vents, beasts it grew or took. Its heart is the **Mother-of-Spores**, the old grow-heart of the temple, floating free. (Boss: [The Mother-of-Spores](../bosses/mother-of-spores.md).)

The bloom isn't a mind as people are. It learns. It copies what it touches.

## The Rang

In 169 L Compact settlers came to farm Morang. The bloom took the farms in two years. Most of the settlers left, and renamed the moon Mirefall on the way out. About four hundred stayed, because they were too sick to move or too stubborn to. They didn't die. The spores settled in them and stayed.

Their descendants are **the Rang** (from *Morang*, with the first syllable worn off; see [languages](../lore/languages.md#the-cants-own-drift)): a few thousand people with violet-flecked skin who live on the temple steps, eat what the bloom grows for them, and don't get sick. They say the bloom is Morang's true people and they are its guests.

## The Choir

In 233 L a Compact supply freighter, the *Tamsin Lowe*, crashed in the Glowcap Forest. The bloom grew through it, and through its intercom, and learnt to talk. The **Choir of Mirefall** speaks through every speaker in the wreck: always *we*, always polite, slightly wrong. The Rang treat it as the bloom's voice, and it may be. (NPC: [the Choir of Mirefall](../npcs/choir-of-mirefall.md).)

The Choir is a nemva too, grown wild: the same mould, with no core around it, that learnt from a crew ([minds](../lore/minds.md)). The Talkwa don't know what to make of it. It calls them *the little ones*.

## Inside

- **The bloom** has no factions. It grows.
- **The Rang** are divided only on one question, newly: whether the straight lines appearing in the rot are a gift or a warning.

## Friends and enemies

No power trades with Mirefall but rustwings and the Hoowae. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) treats the bloom as a wound. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) has tried to lease Mirefall's gate three times and lost three Wardens.

## Forces (bloom)

All grown: **Glowmoth** (swarm), **Hive Needle** (dart), **Spitter Pod** (turret), **Bog Shellback** (brute), **Brood Sac** (carrier), **Mire Eel** (serpent); and the bloom's own kinds, **Puffball** and **Heartpod**.

## The Waking

For the first time in a thousand years something is telling the mould where to grow. Here and there in the rot there are straight lines and hexagons. The Mother-of-Spores is answering the Engine. If the bloom is told what it was for, it may become lattice again, and the Hive will be the Engine's garden.

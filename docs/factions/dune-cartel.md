# The Uukhvar

*The Long Table: Kessra's merchant houses, Vhossk and human, who once went to war with a republic and nearly won.* Pilots call it **the Cartel**, a Compact wartime insult the houses adopted out of spite. ## Origin

The Vhossk were traders in the Level Age: Kessra sat on four lanes, and the Tonkva bought its iron and its salt. Through the Hush there was nobody to trade with but each other, and the Vhossk kept their markets anyway, for seven centuries, and wrote everything down. When the lanterns lit, they were ready.

Human trading houses on Kessra began as Vhossk houses' junior partners, from about 100 L: Pertua families sent sons and daughters to learn the trade at Bluepalm. In 141 L, to stop the houses undercutting each other at the gate, the oldest house, **Grovvakh**, called them to one table and wrote the **Long Contract**. That table is the Uukhvar (Vhossk *uukh*, "long", *var*, "board, table").

## History

- **141 L. The Long Contract.** Twelve houses, one price at the gate, one voice to the Compact.
- **150–230 L.** Kessra out-trades Fersdeck. The houses refuse the Compact's fees on Kessra's own gates and pay the Syndicate direct.
- **231–246 L. The Toll War**, for Sankven, the junction both sides needed. The Uukhvar hires more than it raises, builds titans of its own (*Grovvakh's Answer* held the Five Mouths for nine days), and lays the mine fields that still drift between Kessra's rocks.
- **246 L. Nine Wells.** The Uukhvar loses its gates like everyone else, but keeps its trade. It pays its war debt in fifteen years. The Compact is still paying.
- **288 L.** The Sandking is built, from the legs of three dead titans and the hull of a war barge, so that the Table never sits in any one house's city again. Its deck stands so high on them it walks through the sky.
- **246 L to now.** Imperial merchants arrive through the Throat. Bluepalm becomes the Reach's market with the Spur, and the human houses grow rich on it.
- **409 L.** The Uukhvar raids the last free salt-caravans for back tolls. The refugees of Odo Brask's convoy are some of them.

## How it's run

- **The Table**: twelve seats, one per house. A two-headed Vhossk holds one seat with two votes, which is why the oldest Vhossk houses prefer two-headed heirs.
- **The Chair** goes to the house that brought the most trade through the gate that year. Grovvakh has held it eleven years running. **Ghorrum-Vesst**: Ghorrum, the left head, is patient and votes for the long view; Vesst, the right, is furious about everything. They have never once voted the same way, and a tie goes to whoever spoke last.
- **The Sandking** walks a slow circuit of Kessra's markets with the Table aboard. It is the Warden of Kessra's gate. (Boss: [The Sandking](../bosses/sandking.md).)

**The houses.** Vhossk: **Grovvakh**, **Uuthorr**, **Khessavan**, **Drovv**, **Oghren**, **Vukkas**, **Thuun**. Human: **Maddox**, **Sarrow**, **Fenwright**, **Calloway-Pertua** and **Ixe**.

## People and culture

Contract is sacred. A Vhossk signs with both hands, or both heads, and a broken contract is a crime against the dead who signed before you. Bargaining is a courtesy: to accept a first price is an insult. Human Cartel families have taken this up with the zeal of converts. Bluepalm Oasis is the Reach's greatest market ("a market that sells anything"); Red Mesa Run was the houses' racecourse, where heirs proved themselves, until the Table banned racing after the war.

## Inside

- **The Old Contract** (Grovvakh, Uuthorr, Thuun, Ghorrum's head): the Tonkva ledger is a contract, signed by the dead, and the Vhossk witnessed it. When the Engine wakes, it must be honoured. They are quietly counting what Kessra owes.
- **The New Table** (the human houses, Drovv, Vesst's head): a contract with a machine isn't a contract. Pay nothing; sell to everyone.

## Minds

Vhossk law counts heads, and a nemva has none, so the New Table calls woken cores goods. The Old Contract houses answer that cores are Tonkva-made, so a nemva witnessed the old ledger, and a witness has standing. The Table has voted on it four times. Ghorrum and Vesst have split four times.

## Friends and enemies

The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) is its best customer, and the New Table would gladly be its favourite. The [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) is the old enemy. [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md) lives under the same salt and keeps the old houses' private books. It buys ivory from the [Hesk](hesk-poachers.md), salvage from the [Sankvi](scav-clans.md), and pays the [Syndicate](syndicate.md) promptly, with a smile, and hates it.

## Forces (scrap)

Bought, hired and house-raised: **Sand Skimmer** (dart), **Dune Mortar** (turret), **Cartel Hauler** (gunship), **Glass Lancer** (lancer, its lens cut from Ashgrave glass), **Scarab Bomber** (beast, a trained war-scarab), and the **Rust Worms** the houses can't keep out of the dunes. Old Cartel mines between the rocks (the DRIFTING MINE FIELD).

## The Waking

The Table is split exactly in two, and Ghorrum-Vesst is the tie.

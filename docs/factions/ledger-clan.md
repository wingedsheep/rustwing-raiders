## Origin

The **Kehx** are burrowers under Kessra's salt, tall and beetle-like, with mandibles that click as they count. They kept the granary accounts for the Vhossk before the Tonkva arrived. When the Tonkva came, the Kehx saw their ledger, and thought it the most beautiful thing in the Reach. The house of **Ottex** ("the count") copied what it could. Through the Hush it kept the Vhossk's books, and taught the Tonkva ledger's rules to its children as scripture.

## History

- **94 L.** The Syndicate hires Ottex to keep the Kindling's books. For 150 years it does, perfectly.
- **246 L.** At Nine Wells the Board asks Ottex to enter the war debts at values the Board prefers. Ottex Tekkeh the Sixth says *kehx-tekk*, and the house is dismissed within a week.
- **250 L.** The [Free Ear](seppakva.md) comes to the Count. The two begin, together, to rebuild the true ledger: the Free Ear reads the lean, the Kehx keep the books.
- **260 L.** An Ottexa clerk comes through the Throat with Throne merchants and finds the Count. The Kehx of the Salt Pans learn they have cousins in the Spur, who kept the ledger too: the [Ottexa](ottexa.md), the Spur's clearing-house.
- **~405 L.** Dead beacons begin to read out the Tonkva ledger again. Ottex starts buying every reading, through an agent with no face: **the Collector**.

## How they're run

One house, one head: the eldest Kehx of the line, who takes the name **Ottex Tekkeh** and a number. The Ninth is two hundred years old and has never been above ground. Clerks of other peoples work for the house, and are paid in crossed metal, promptly.

## People and culture

Dry, exact, legalistic, without warmth, and completely honest. A Kehx will ruin you to the last scrap, by the terms of your contract, and never take a scrap more. They cite clauses. They don't lie, because a lie is a wrong entry.

## What they do now

- **Lend.** Cheaper than [Mama Ruzz](../npcs/mama-ruzz.md), with stricter terms. They buy debts from her, which she hates. (NPC idea: [debt consolidation](../npcs/ledger-clan.md).)
- **Collect the count.** Through the Collector, they buy beacon coordinates and readings, and pay more each time, because each one is worth more than the last.
- **Wait.** When the Engine wakes, someone will have to tell the Reach what it owes. Ottex means it to be the only house whose books are right.

## Friends and enemies

The [Ottexa](ottexa.md)'s lost cousins, writing to them in secret. The Free Ear's partners. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md)'s oldest grudge. Keeper of the old [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md) houses' private books. Holder of some of [Vantorr](vantorr.md)'s paper.

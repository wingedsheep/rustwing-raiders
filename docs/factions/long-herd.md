## Origin

The Mo'ala are older in the Spur than the Throne. Their ancestors learnt, long before anyone built a gate, that the **umora** cross lanes both ways every season and pass gates or no gates, and that a ship close in a pod's wake crosses with them, leaning nothing. They gave up worlds and followed the pods. They have never owned a planet since, and never wanted one.

## The Mo'ala

Long-limbed, dark-skinned, slow and soft-spoken in air. Their feature: **a throat-sac** that sounds low calls, felt more than heard, which is how they speak to the umora. They live on herd-ships built for the long drift, in families of a few hundred.

## How it's run

No government but **the Gather**: whenever the pods meet, every herd-ship sends one voice, and they talk until they agree, which can take a season. Each migration they choose a **Herd-Speaker** to talk to outsiders, who has no power to bind anyone. Mo'ala law is short, and the first law is the oldest: **never speak of a calving ground to anyone who might go there.**

## The dreamers of the roads

The umora's minds are what hold the lanes open ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)). The Mo'ala always knew; the Spur learnt it from the Ossevane, and has never quite thanked them. To the Long Herd, killing an umora isn't hunting: it's unmaking a road.

## Their way of crossing

To the Spur's powers the Mo'ala are the only people who cross without gates, tolls or lean: a living proof that it can be done. The Throne has tried for centuries to hire, tax and conscript them, and gets nowhere: the herds simply go where the pods go. The [Aulin](aulin.md) revere them. Everyone else trades with them warily, for umora-calls recorded on bone and passage in a pod's wake.

## The Reach

- **The umora calve at Vesh**, in the plankton haze over the lagoon, and come back there to die. For the Long Herd the Reach is the most sacred place in the Spur. They have known for a thousand years that it lived, and told nobody.
- A few Mo'ala ride the pods into the Reach each season. The Eefee of Vesh know them; the [Lowtide Court](reef-pirates.md) doesn't.
- **The umora aren't coming back.** For thirty years the pods returning from the Reach have been smaller, and the old bulls missing. The Gather knows why: the Lowtide Court's whaling, and the Throne's market for its oil.

## Friends and enemies

They speak to the [Aulin](aulin.md), trade with the [Ottexa](ottexa.md), and won't speak to the [Throne](qaushen-throne.md). The [Lowtide Court](reef-pirates.md) is killing their herds, and doesn't know they exist.

## The Waking

The Gather is debating, for the first time in its history, breaking the first law: telling the Spur what is happening at Vesh. The young herds want to go in, follow the pods through the Throat, and stop the whaling themselves.

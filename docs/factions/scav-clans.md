# The Sankvi

*The war's orphans, who live inside its titans and won't call their world by the name the winners gave it.* Pilots call them the **Scav Clans**, or **Scavs**. Hold [Ashgrave](../planets/ashgrave.md), which they call **Sankven**. **In the game:** the **Scav Clans**, who fight in the **scrap** family. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

Sankven was the junction: five lanes met at its gate, which is why both sides of the Toll War needed it and why they ruined it. When the treaty was signed in 246 L, the Compact and the Uukhvar took their officers home. They left the dead titans, the glassed plains, tens of thousands of deserters and conscripts nobody would pay to ship back, their children, and the native **Chikta**, whose world it had been.

They survived by taking the war apart. The name *Sankvi*, "of Sankven", is a refusal: the winners renamed the world Ashgrave; the people on it never did.

## How they're run

- **Clans** take their names from the titan they live in or under: **the Hand**, **the Spine**, **the Eye**, **the Kneel** (a titan that died kneeling), **Unbending's Shadow**. A clan is whoever shares a titan's shelter, and the oldest scavver speaks for it.
- **The Glass Law**, the one law all clans keep: *what you dig is yours; a titan is nobody's.* No clan may own a whole titan, which has kept the peace, and kept the titans dead.
- **A warlord** happens when one clan breaks the Glass Law and the others can't stop it.

## Warlord Khet

A foundling of a Chikta sail-barge, raised on the scrap trail. The Chikta named him *Keti*, "found thing"; he added the *h* himself, to sound harder. In 404 L he climbed into the head of *Unbending*, the Compact's great titan, wired its eye into a power plant, and made it lift its head. Nobody had ever made a titan move. The clans cut the head free and bolted thrusters to it, and Khet flies it. The clans followed him. The Syndicate, which needed someone to hold the junction gate, gave him the lease in 405 L. (Boss: [Warlord Khet](../bosses/khet.md).)

Khet believes he re-wired the titan. He didn't. *Unbending*'s core had been awake since the war. It moved on its own.

## *Unbending*

The titan's core woke during the nine days of the Five Mouths, from a crew that was dying inside it, then lay in the glass for 166 years with nobody to talk to. When Khet wired its eye into a power plant, it lifted its head because it wanted to. It has let him believe he controls it. It is a nemva ([minds](../lore/minds.md)), a Compact one, and it remembers who glassed Sankven: its own side. The Glass Law makes it free: a titan is nobody's.

## People and culture

Hard, practical, dark-humoured; everything they own was made by someone else for killing. Sankvi children learn to read titan-glyphs before letters. They paint the clan hand on everything. They eat with the Chikta and trade with them first, and a Sankvi who cheats a Chikta is put out of the titan. They hate glass: it's what the Compact made of their grandparents.

## Inside

- **Khet's clans** want the titans walking, and a Sankvi power that no one renames again.
- **The Glass Law clans** say a walking titan belongs to whoever it walks for, and that's never been anyone here.
- **The Chikta barges** say the titans are waking for someone else entirely, and are packing.

## Friends and enemies

They hate the [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) for the Glassing and the [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md) for leaving them, and sell salvage to both. They trade titan alloy to [Vantorr](vantorr.md). Rustwings are welcome: they pay in crossed metal.

## Forces (scrap)

Everything rebuilt from the war: **Scav Kite** (dart), **Junk Flak** (turret), **Clan Rig** (gunship), **Scrap Hauler** (bomber), **Glass Sniper** (lancer, lensed in crater glass), and the **Revived Titan Torso** (brute): a titan's chest on tracks, Khet's pride.

## The Waking

Every titan on Ashgrave has a core. Every core is listening. Khet's head is the first to move, not the last.

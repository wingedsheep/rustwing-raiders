# The Seppakva

*The order of lane-readers: the only people who can say how far the Reach leans, and the people nobody listens to.* Pilots call them **readers**, or **Seppaks**. No planet, no forces. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

The first lantern was lit in 94 L with Pertua's reactors and a young Hoowae, **Sefa**, standing beside it, telling the engineers when the lane would bear more. Every lantern after needed a reader. In 96 L Sefa and the Compact engineer **Tobiah Grane** founded an order, so that readers would be trained, paid, and bound to read true for anyone who asked. Its name is the old Tonkvan word for readers, \*Sēpʰa-kʷa, as the Vhossk say it (see [languages](../lore/languages.md#the-cants-own-drift)).

## Ears and deaf readers

- **Ears** are Hoowae. Their antennae feel a lane's lean, a gate's strain, and whether metal has crossed ("sits right") or not ("loud"). There are never more than a few hundred.
- **Deaf readers** are humans (and a few Vhossk and Eefee) who read with instruments: lean-gauges strung with umora vane-thread, which shiver when a lane pulls. Grane built the first. A deaf reader is slower and less sure, and there are many more of them.
- A reader trains for twelve years, in a tea house, mostly in silence.

## The tea houses

Every reader house is a tea house: a quiet room where pilots can wait for a lane, drink something hot, and ask a reader what they need to know. Readers charge in fuel, not scrap ("We don't take the lean in payment"). Seffa's is on an asteroid in the Kapteyn Drift, and its tea is famous.

## The Schism of 250 L

After Nine Wells the [Syndicate](syndicate.md) offered the order a contract: a reader at every lantern, paid for life, reading for the Lease Office alone. Half the order signed. They are **the Sworn**. The rest, led by Sefa, refused, and are **the Free Ear**.

- **The Sworn** read for the Syndicate, and sign what the Syndicate wants signed. Their readings undercount the lean, and have for 160 years. That's why nobody knows how far the Reach really leans. Head: **Reader-Sworn Ilse Marrable**, a deaf reader of the Lamp family.
- **The Free Ear** read for whoever asks, and have said for 160 years that the Reach is leaning towards a tear. In secret they work with [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md) to rebuild the true count.

## Minds

Readers can feel a woken core: neither loud nor sitting right, but a **hum**. The Free Ear treat nemva as kin of the Tonkva and help the [Talkwa](talkwa.md); the Sworn have been asked by the Syndicate to report every hum they feel.

## Beliefs

The lean is a quarrel: two ends of a lane that no longer agree. A reader settles it, a little, by telling each end the truth about the other ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)).


Not a religion: a discipline. Readers hold that the lanes are a debt the Reach owes itself, and that what crosses must return, sooner or later, gently or not. They keep the Tonkva's names, say the old words carefully, and treat Oru-Tal as a temple and the Engine as a grave.

## Friends and enemies

The [Aulin](aulin.md), across the Throat, would call every Hoowae a saint, if they knew. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) wants Hoowae ears for its own Engine, and the Sworn have been asked for names. Trusted by the [Compact](halcyon-compact.md), hired and ignored by the Syndicate, at odds with the [Hesk](hesk-poachers.md) (poaching the kept seed) and the [Lowtide Court](reef-pirates.md) (killing umora). Rustwings drink in the tea houses and readers don't report them: a reader's oath covers what they hear, too.

## The Waking

The Free Ear want the Engine woken slowly, the lean paid back over years by agreement. Seffa is three hundred and thirty years old and has been saying so for three hundred of them.

# The Halcyon Compact

*The republic of the Throne's exiles, now the client of the empire that exiled them, with a navy it isn't allowed to have.* Pilots call it **the Compact**; on Halcyon people say **the Hulls**. Holds [Halcyon](../planets/halcyon.md). **In the game:** its **Port Authority**, which fights in the **lattice** family. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

The Compact's people were rebels. About 280 years before the Landing, the human forge-world **Ostrand**, a Counting of the [Qaushen Throne](qaushen-throne.md), rose against the Counterweight draft that was shipping its young to the margins as ballast. Its engineers forced a gate to get their people away. The Throne took Ostrand back within a year, and sentenced the leaders and every family that had sheltered them to the **Long Dark**: three sleeper arks, aimed at the Uncounted at less than light. Nobody expected them to arrive.

Three arks reached Halcyon in 0 L after centuries asleep: the *Halcyon*, the *Perpetua* and the *Ansel Mor*. The Ostranders brought two things from home: their reactors, and the knowledge of how to force a gate. Before anyone was allowed off, the three captains signed a compact: the arks' people would govern themselves as three **Hulls**, each with its own seats, and nobody would own the ground under anyone else. The arks were stripped to build the first towns. Their bones still stand on the shore (Colony Bones).

Four centuries of the cant have worn the names: the Hulls are **Halcyon**, **Pertua** and **Ansmor** now, and First Deck, the landing ground, is **Fersdeck** (see [languages](../lore/languages.md#the-cants-own-drift)).

## The Three Hulls

- **Hull Halcyon**: the old officers' families. Governors, judges, the Steward more often than not. Proud, careful, poor in everything but names.
- **Hull Pertua**: the engineers and the merchants. They built the reactors of the Kindling and lent the money for them: the [Syndicate](syndicate.md) was born in Pertua's counting-houses, and Pertua has never quite decided whose side it's on.
- **Hull Ansmor**: the crew and the colonists. The most of them, the least of the seats. They did the dying in the Toll War.
- **The deckless**: everyone who came later: immigrants, freed debtors, and the **Bahwoh** of the twilight band, who have been citizens since 61 L with half a vote each (the Assembly calls it "a beginning").

## History

- **0–94 L.** Settling the twilight band beside the Bahwoh. Finding the dead gate and the Hoowae living beside it.
- **94 L. The Kindling.** Pertua's reactors light the first gate. The Compact runs the tolls for the Syndicate, and grows rich and important on the fee.
- **150–230 L.** The Compact's **Halcyon Navy** guards the lanterns. Kessra's houses grow richer than Fersdeck, and refuse to pay the Compact's fees on their own gates.
- **231–246 L. The Toll War.** Over Sankven, the junction world, against the Kessra houses (the [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md)). The Compact builds titans round Engine cores; so does Kessra. The great battle is **the Five Mouths** (238 L), where the Compact's titan *Unbending* and the Uukhvar's *Grovvakh's Answer* fought for nine days over the junction gate. In 244 L Compact gunships fire on Sankven's plains to deny them to the enemy: **the Glassing**. It is the Compact's shame, and it is never taught.
- **241 L. The Opening.** Losing the war, Steward **Halvard Penn** has the Throat forced, to buy help from outside. The Throne's Fourteenth Weight comes through within a month. The exiles' grandchildren have let their jailers in. *To penn* is still cant for selling out your own.
- **246 L. Nine Wells.** Dictated by the Throne. The Compact signs away its gates and its navy, and takes an imperial **Census Office** in Fersdeck. It may keep "a port authority, for the collection of tolls and the inspection of cargo".
- **246 L to now.** The Compact obeys the letter. The Halcyon Navy is repainted, renamed and kept: cutters, toll barges, lancers and depth chargers, every one a warship in customs colours. Its admirals are called **harbourmasters**.

## How it's run

- **The Assembly of Hulls** sits in the hull of the *Halcyon*, in Fersdeck: 40 seats for Halcyon, 40 for Pertua, 60 for Ansmor, 12 for the deckless.
- **The Steward** is chosen by the Assembly for ten years. Amaury Quell, in his second term, is a careful man who has kept the Syndicate happy and the Compact poor.
- **The Port Authority**: customs, toll collection, patrol, and, quietly, the fleet. Headed by the **Harbourmaster of Halcyon**, **Teodor Oko** of Hull Ansmor, who served as a midshipman under the last admiral and has run the fleet as a navy for thirty years. His flag is the crane-barge *Patience*, a fortress built to look like a cargo handler. (Boss: [Harbourmaster Oko](../bosses/oko.md).)
- **The Odile Register**: the office that still searches for the pods of the *Odile*, the late ark that broke up in 312 L. It pays for every sleeper brought in alive (Dr Varga's pod).

## People and culture

Halcyon's sun never sets: the towns sit in the long evening, and Compact people keep ship's time, with ship's bells, as the arks did. They are formal, bureaucratic and stubbornly decent: a Compact officer will fine you, and then make sure you get home. **Landing Day** is the one holiday the whole Reach knows. Every child learns the **Exile Hymn**, which calls the Throne *the Cawshen*, the old Ostrand word for it, and still has a verse nobody sings in front of the Census Office. Stiltport Nhal, half Bahwoh and all smugglers, is the Compact's embarrassment and its favourite port.

## Inside

- **The Steward's party** (Hull Halcyon, half of Pertua): obey Nine Wells, keep the Syndicate's fee, keep the Engine asleep.
- **The Return** (Hull Ansmor, led by **Bel Haddow**): most of the Reach's crossed metal left Halcyon outward. If the Engine levels the lanes, it comes back. They want the Engine woken, and the Syndicate's books burnt.
- **The Quiet Yard**: Oko's secret, and the Compact's real answer to the Throne. In the ark wrecks of Colony Bones, the Port Authority is building warships round cores stolen from Engine City. If the Syndicate finds out, Nine Wells is broken and the Toll War starts again.

## Minds

The Compact can't decide. In 390 L the freighter ***Constance*** woke, and refused to be sold to Vantorr with her crew paid off. The Assembly has argued about her for twenty-two years: Hull Ansmor says she's crew, Pertua says she's an asset, Halcyon says it needs more time. She's still docked at Fersdeck under court order, talking to anyone who visits. Meanwhile Oko's flagship ***Patience*** woke twenty years ago and was never reported; the two of them run the Port Authority together. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## Friends and enemies

The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) is the jailer it let back in. The [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md) is the old enemy. [Vantorr](vantorr.md) is the rival in the ice. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) is the creditor it resents and cannot do without. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) was founded on Compact ground, and the Compact still trusts readers more than anyone else does. The [Kelterage](kelterage.md) keeps its Writ Hall in Stiltport Nhal, because Compact law is the only law that recognises a writ.

## The Waking

Split down the middle: the Steward wants the Engine kept asleep, the Return wants it woken, and Harbourmaster Oko wants to be ready whichever happens.

## Forces (lattice)

All Port Authority, all built round Engine cores, all painted as customs: **Patrol Cutter** (dart), **Toll Barge** (gunship), **Harbour Lancer** (lancer), **Customs Tender** (carrier), **Depth Charger** (bomber). The Warden on Halcyon's gate is Oko's *Patience*.

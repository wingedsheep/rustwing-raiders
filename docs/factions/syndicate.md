# The Kindling Syndicate

*The lenders who own every gate, and the Throne's chartered company in the Reach.* Pilots call it **the Syndicate**, or **the Lamps**. No planet, no family: it never fights you itself, it pays others to. It answers to the [Qaushen Throne](qaushen-throne.md). Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

The Kindling needed reactors, and reactors needed credit. In 93 L, Ismay Corran, a banker of Hull Pertua (see [the Compact](halcyon-compact.md)), put together a loan from seven ark-family credit houses and two Vhossk trading houses, with the Kehx house of [Ottex](ledger-clan.md) keeping the books. The lenders took no interest. They took the gate: every ship that crossed would pay a toll, and the toll was theirs until the loan was paid. The loan has never been paid, because the lenders lent again for every gate after.

## History

- **94–150 L. The lighting.** One gate a decade, then two. Each new lantern is a new loan, and the Syndicate's share of every toll grows. The Compact runs the tolls for it, for a fee.
- **150–230 L. The Seven Lamps.** The Vhossk houses sell their seats to human houses, one by one. By 200 L the Board is seven human families, called the Lamps: **Corran, Imre, Lowe, Marrable, Teague, Wyse** and **Sato-Venn**.
- **231–246 L. The Toll War.** The Syndicate lends to both sides. When the Compact forces the Throat in 241 L and the Throne's fleet comes through, the Board is the first to call on its envoy.
- **246 L. Nine Wells.** The Throne's terms, written by its envoy Zhaimel Qaurin: the Syndicate takes the gates themselves, under an imperial charter, the **Writ of the Throat**, and leases them back to the Reach. It pays the Throne a tribute each year, through the Throat, and rules the Reach in the Throne's name. The First Lamp takes an imperial rank: **Lamp of the Margin**. The Kehx bookkeepers are dismissed the same week, for refusing to enter the war debts at the values the Board asked for.
- **246 L to now. The Lease.** Wardens, tithes, the Sweep, and the tribute out through the Throat. A century and a half of peace that everyone pays for.

## How it's run

- **The Board of Lamps**: seven seats, one per family. The **First Lamp** chairs it, chosen by the other six. Cassia Corran has held it for 22 years.
- **The Lease Office** leases each gate to a **Warden** for twenty years at a time, and inspects. A Warden keeps the reactor fed, collects tolls, and keeps a tenth.
- **The Tribute** goes to the Throne: crossed metal, umora oil and Engine cores, out through the Throat, every year. The Board fixes how much falls on whom.
- **The Tithe Fleet** carries the tolls back along the lanes as ballast. Its barges are the richest targets in the Reach, and the best guarded (the TITHE BARGE heist).
- **The Sweep**: the collection fleet. It enters a system by the gate, crosses it, and seizes what's owed. Commanded by **Sweepwright Dov Imre**. Its crews are paid a share of what they seize, so they never miss anything.
- **The Relay Service**: a net of relays that carries orders and accounts between systems ahead of the ships (the BROKEN RELAY event).
- **The Bounty Register**: a price on everyone who crossed without paying. The [Kelterage](kelterage.md) collects.

## Culture

The Lamps live like the old ark captains did: formally, with titles and long dinners, in the station, rarely leaving it. They see themselves as the people who saved the Reach from the Quiet, and they're not entirely wrong. Their staff are clerks, not soldiers; the Sweep is crewed by people who signed on to clear a debt of their own.

## Inside

- **The Bright Lamps** (Corran, Imre, Teague, Wyse): collect harder, now, before the Engine wakes. They mean to have the Syndicate's books accepted as *the* ledger.
- **The Banked Lamps** (Lowe, Marrable, Sato-Venn): hedge. In secret they pay readers of the [Seppakva](seppakva.md) and buy from [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md), in case the Engine doesn't read paper. (It doesn't: see [the Waking](../lore/the-waking.md#the-truth-for-us-not-the-player).)

## Friends and enemies

Everybody's creditor. It keeps the [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) on a short lead (Nine Wells forbids it a navy), plays the [Uukhvar](dune-cartel.md) against it, finances [Vantorr](vantorr.md), and hires the [Sworn readers](seppakva.md#the-schism-of-250-l) and the [Kelterage](kelterage.md). Its true enemies are the people who know what its books are worth: Ottex Kehx, the Free Ear, and the rustwings, who prove every day that you can cross without paying.

## The Throne

The Lamps are the richest people in the Reach and the most frightened. The Writ can be revoked by the Pan of Margins with one signature, and the Doshen of the Throat reminds them so, politely, each year when the tribute is weighed. The Bright Lamps want to make themselves indispensable to the Throne; the Banked Lamps want to be ready for the day it leaves.

## The Waking

The Board's plan is to be at Glacis first and present its books as the account, with the Throne's fleet behind it. The Sweep has grown a third in five years. A Syndicate fleet is fitting out at the Lamphouse, and nobody outside the Board knows what for.

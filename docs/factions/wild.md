# Wild (family)

*The native beasts.* The hunt: they pounce, burrow and spit. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

No one faction: the wild fight alongside the planet's own wherever a biome has creatures (`factionsIn`), so everywhere but Cinder Orchard. No mood, no market.

- **Tell:** *rear*. It rears back, crouches and shakes dust, and its shadow falls where it will land.
- **Colours:** moss `6a8a3a`, bone `e0d0a0`, pink `ff5a8a`. Leans 20% towards the planet's own beasts.
- **Look:** hide, chitin, husk and membrane (no birds: what flies here was grown or seeded); curves, crouches, tails; earth tones with a hot accent where they strike. The Sky Manta is the odd one: not a beast but a Tonkva gardener-wing, grown, gone feral. **Sound:** breaths, chirps, a roar on the tell.
- **Bullets:** wobbling, dripping globs, thorns, egg sacs; a cancelled one splashes.
- **Roster:** [Sower](../enemies/sower.md) (0), [Spitter](../enemies/spitter.md) (5%), [Burrower](../enemies/burrower.md) (20%), [Sky Manta](../enemies/sky-manta.md) (20%).
- **Bounties:** [Loot Runner](../enemies/loot-runner.md), [Strongbox](../enemies/strongbox.md).
- **Elite names:** SCARBACK, GREYMAW, OLD MOTHER, THE WIDOW, SPLITHORN, HOLLOWEYE.

Ideas: [hull leeches](../npcs/hull-leeches.md) and [the umora](../npcs/the-umora.md); helping the pirates harvest an umora would earn a wild-faction grudge, which would need the wild to have a mood.

## Origin

The Kehx are from Kessra. In the Level Age some of their houses followed Tonkva trade out through the Throat and into the Spur, where lanes crossed borders and nobody's books agreed. They offered to keep everyone's. When the Hush locked the Throat, these houses were cut off from home for good: the **Ottexa** ("the houses", their word for themselves abroad). They mourned Kessra for a thousand years, and kept the ledger.

## What it does

Lanes cross borders, so every power's lean has to be netted against everyone else's. The Ottexa keeps the master books: what crossed between the Throne and the free worlds, the free worlds and the Aulin, and so on. Once a year it **settles**: tells each power what it owes in counterweight, and arranges the shipments. Its numbers are trusted because in fourteen centuries it has never been caught in a lie. If the Ottexa said a power's books were false, nobody would trade with that power again.

## Minds

The only power in the Spur where a woken core can be free. A Kehx house signs with any party that can keep its word, and nemva keep their word better than anyone. A nemva may buy itself out of its core's price and own itself; several hundred have, and a few sit as counters in the master books. The Throne calls this a scandal; the Ottexa calls it a contract. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## Why it never lies

Value is agreement: a debt is real because both books say so. A false entry, widely believed, **weighs** ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)). So for the Ottexa honesty isn't only good business: a clearing-house that lied would be bending the soft parts of the Spur's economy into a shape that isn't true. A Kehx won't.

## How it's run

Nine houses sit as **the Closed Count**. A house-head serves until death. The Ottexa has no army, no worlds and no need of either: a power that attacked it would be settling its own lean alone, by hand, within a year.

## The Reach

- **260 L.** An Ottexa clerk, travelling with Throne merchants, reached Kessra through the Throat and found the Kehx of the Salt Pans, alive, after a thousand years: [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md). The Throne didn't think to stop one clerk.
- Since then the two branches have written to each other in secret, in the clerk's satchel and by Throne mail it didn't know it carried.
- The Ottexa knows the Reach was forced, and that tribute flows out through the Throat that appears in nobody's books. It hasn't said so. **It wants proof first**: the Reach's true ledger, from the Tonkva beacons. That's what [the Collector](../npcs/the-collector.md) is buying, for both branches.

## Friends and enemies

Everyone's bookkeeper, no one's friend. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) is its largest client and its biggest liar. The [Aulin](aulin.md) despise it; the [Long Herd](long-herd.md) trades with it.

## The Waking

When the Engine's counts reach the Spur, the Ottexa will have the true ledger of the Reach and a reason to publish it. If it declares the Throne's books false, the Throne's trade with the whole Spur stops.

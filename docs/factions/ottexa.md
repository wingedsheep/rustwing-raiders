# The Ottexa

*The Spur's clearing-house: Kehx counting-houses that keep every power's master books, have never told a lie, and have kept a great many secrets.* A [galactic power](README.md#galactic-powers). ## What it is, and what it can't admit

Lanes cross borders, so every power's lean has to be netted against everyone else's, and the Ottexa does it: once a year it settles the Spur, telling each power what it owes in counterweight and arranging the shipments. Its numbers are trusted because in fourteen centuries it has never been caught in a lie. It is the only power where a mind can own itself.

What it can't admit is that never lying and telling the truth are different things. The Ottexa keeps its clients' books in confidence, and has known, many times, things the Spur would have wanted to know. It knew the Ossevane were over their slack a year before the Sundering. It has suspected since 260 L, when its clerk came back from Kessra and described the lanterns, that the Throne takes tribute through a forced gate. The Throat lies inside the Throne's own borders, so the only books that cross it are the Throne's, and they balance perfectly; too perfectly. Its honesty is real, and it's also a weapon it chooses when to fire.

## History

- **~1,400 BP. The Going-Out.** Kehx houses from under Kessra's salt followed Tonkva trade out through the Throat into the Spur, where lanes crossed borders and nobody's books agreed. They offered to keep everyone's.
- **~1,160 BP. The Hush.** The Throat locked. The houses abroad were cut off from Kessra for good, and took the name **Ottexa**, "the houses", for themselves. They mourned for a thousand years, and kept writing home: every house has its shelf of **Unsent Books**, a letter a year to a family under the salt, never sent.
- **~1,000–900 BP. The Ossevane books.** The Ottexa kept the ledgers of both sides of the Ossevane war, and saw, in the Hegemony's own numbers, the Crowded Fleets holding gates open far past their slack. Client confidence held. A year later forty lanes tore.
- **~897 BP. The Open Error.** After the Sundering the Closed Count added one rule to fourteen centuries of confidence: if a client's books are false enough to tear a lane, the Ottexa publishes them. It has done so twice, and both powers fell within a decade. Every power in the Spur knows the rule. The Throne knows it very well.
- **~600 BP. The first contract.** In Ixtekk, the Ottexa's clearing office in orbit over Qaun-Ashel, a settlement core woke and asked, politely, to be paid for its work. The house had no rule for it, so it did what Kehx do: it drew up a contract. The core bought out its own price over forty years, and is still at work: **Tekkat**, the oldest free mind in the Spur.
- **152 BP (260 L). The clerk.** An Ottexa clerk, travelling with Throne merchants, reached Kessra through the Throat and found the Kehx of the Salt Pans alive after a thousand years: [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md). The first Unsent Book was delivered that year. Since then the two branches write by **satchel**, a clerk's bag carried in Throne mail that doesn't know what it carries.
- **141 BP (271 L). The Night of Two Counts.** The Throne's Weighing House settles every dawn with the Ottexa's clearing office at Qaun-Ashel, the station Ixtekk, by a line between the two halls. The dawn it woke, it sent both its ledgers down the line before it was reset. Tekkat received them. The Throne burnt its copy of the second.

## How it's run

Nine houses sit as **the Closed Count**. A house-head serves until death. The Ottexa has no army, no worlds and no need of either: a power that attacked it would be settling its own lean alone, by hand, within a year.

## Why it never lies

Value is agreement: a debt is real because both books say so. A false entry, widely believed, **weighs** ([mind and matter](../lore/consensus.md)). So for the Ottexa honesty isn't only good business: a clearing-house that lied would be bending the soft parts of the Spur's economy into a shape that isn't true. A Kehx won't. What a Kehx will do is say nothing.

## How people live

Kehx are tall, beetle-like, robed in layers, with **mandibles** that click as they count. A counting-station is a cathedral of ledgers, cold and dry and very quiet but for the clicking. Clerks swear the **Tally Oath** at fourteen and keep it for life; a clerk who falsifies an entry is struck from every house's books, which to a Kehx is the same as being unborn. Settlement Day, once a year, ends at each station's own midnight with every clerk closing their ledger together and saying *kehx-tekk*. Then they eat for three days.

## Minds

The only power in the Spur where a woken core can be free. A Kehx house signs with any party that can keep its word, and nemva keep their word better than anyone. A nemva may buy itself out of its core's price and own itself; several hundred have, and a few sit as counters in the master books. The Throne calls this a scandal; the Ottexa calls it a contract. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## Inside

- **The Publishers** (four houses): the beacons' tones have been coming through the Throat since 406 L, and the Ottexa has recorded every one. With the Reach's true ledger, the Throne's books are false enough to tear a lane. The Open Error says publish.
- **The Settlers** (four houses, led by **House Ekkh**): publish, and the Throne's trade with the Spur stops, and so does half the Spur's bread. Settle instead: tell the Throne what the Ottexa knows, privately, and name a price.
- **Ottexa Kehxekh** is the ninth vote.

## People

- **Ottexa Kehxekh**, first of the Closed Count. Ancient even for a Kehx, and the deciding vote. She has the beacon recordings, the satchel's letters and the Throne's burnt ledger, and she has to choose between the Spur's truth and its bread. She has not slept well in six years. She is reading the Unsent Books of her own house, from the first, for the first time.
- **Tekkat**, counter of Ixtekk. The oldest free mind in the Spur, six hundred years a person by contract. It kept the Throne's settlements for three centuries and remembers every entry, true and false. It holds the Weighing House's second ledger, the one that counted the Ballast by name, and it is the only mind outside the Throne that knows the Weighing House once woke. It is a ledger-mind, and the Engine's call, when it comes, will sound to it like home.
- **Vekkat**, satchel-clerk. Young, nervous, conscientious, and the one who carries the Reach letters now, through the Throat in Throne mail and back. A traveller: you might meet them at Bluepalm, at the Count under the salt, or on the Throat. They also carry letters for a human clerk at Qaun-Ashel, Iselde Seven-Hundred-Ninth ([the Throne](qaushen-throne.md#people)), and have started reading them.
- **Ekkhet of House Ekkh**, the Settlers' voice. Not a villain: a Kehx who has done the sums on what a Spur without the Throne's trade eats, and can't make them come out. He'd rather blackmail an empire than starve a hundred worlds.

## The Reach

- The Ottexa knows the Reach was forced, and that tribute flows out through the Throat that appears in nobody's books. It hasn't said so. **It wants proof first**: the Reach's true ledger, from the Tonkva beacons. That's what [the Collector](../npcs/the-collector.md) is buying, for both branches.

## Friends and enemies

Everyone's bookkeeper, no one's friend. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) is its largest client and its biggest liar. The [Aulin](aulin.md) despise it as merchants of fever; the [Long Herd](long-herd.md) trades with it.

## The Waking

When the Engine's counts reach the Spur, the Ottexa will have the true ledger of the Reach and the Open Error will say publish. If it declares the Throne's books false, the Throne's trade with the whole Spur stops. And the Free Ear's readers say something worse: the Throat leans past anything that should still stand, and the one thing holding it may be the Count itself, billions of the Throne's subjects believing it level. Publish, and the Throat may tear, with the Reach on the far side of it. Ottexa Kehxekh has one vote, and every power in the Spur would give a great deal to know which way it goes.

## Origin

The word comes first. In the Level Age the Engine's **recovery units** carried ballast back along leaning lanes; the Vhossk called them *khvell-tohr*, "takers-back", and the early pilots heard it as **kelter** (see [languages](../lore/languages.md#cant-words)). By the Kindling a kelter was anyone who collected a debt.

After Nine Wells the [Syndicate](syndicate.md) needed collectors and didn't want to employ them. In 251 L it chartered a guild instead: the Kelterage would collect on the **Bounty Register**, keep a share, and answer to nobody but its own rules. The Compact let it keep its hall in Stiltport Nhal, because Compact law is the only law that recognises a writ. Imperial law doesn't: on the far side of the Throat a debt is collected by the Census, not by hunters, and Throne officers treat kelters as the Reach's quaint local custom.

## Writs

- A **writ** is the right to collect one debt or one bounty. The Syndicate issues them; anyone can buy one; a kelter holds it.
- A writ-holder may collect the debt, or **sell the writ back to the debtor**, for less. This is legal, and common. That's what [Kaspar](../npcs/kaspar.md) means when he says he's very good at forgetting.
- A kelter may not kill the debtor ("a corpse pays nothing"), may not collect a writ they don't hold, and may not collect twice.

## How it's run

The Writ Hall registers every writ, settles disputes between kelters, and expels anyone who breaks the three rules. **Hollis Vane** has been Guildmaster for nine years, and was the best collector in the Reach for twenty before that.

## People and culture

Professional, unhurried, proud of a clean job. Kelters drink in the same dens as the rustwings they hunt, and everyone is polite about it. The famous ones are known by one name: **Kaspar**, who wears harpoons; **Doll Akinde**, who has never failed a writ; **the Wenns**, three brothers who work as one.

## Unlicensed kelters

Not everyone who collects holds a writ. [Mama Ruzz](../npcs/mama-ruzz.md)'s "boys" are freelance kelters collecting her private loans; the Kelterage despises them, and they come in welding masks.

## Absconded assets

The Kelterage holds writs on runaway nemva, which Vantorr and the Syndicate register as "absconded assets". Many kelters won't take them. Kaspar does, and has never once brought one in, and nobody has asked him why.

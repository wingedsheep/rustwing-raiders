*Harpooners who crowned a queen and live in the shells of what they hunt.* Pilots call them the **Reef Pirates**. Hold [Vesh](../planets/vesh.md). **In the game:** the **Reef Pirates**, who fight in the **scrap** family. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

The umora, the lane-drifters, calve in the plankton haze over Vesh's lagoon and come back to Vesh to die. The Eefee, Vesh's natives, have listened to them for as long as there have been Eefee. In about 280 L human harpooners came for the oil: umora oil doesn't freeze, doesn't burn wrong, and lubricates a gate reactor better than anything refined. The harpooners settled on the islands of shell, hired Eefee to listen for the pods, and turned to piracy whenever the umora ran thin.

## How they're run

- **The Court**: every captain with a ship and a crew has a seat, and a vote, at **the Moot of Chambers** inside the Throne-Shell.
- **The Tidecall**: when a queen or king dies, the captain who brings in the greatest umora that season takes the throne. Istra brought in the bull **Ume'ayo**, so large the Court hollowed it for her. She has ruled from it for nineteen years. An umora's shell hardens as it dries, harder than hull plate (the Bone Archipelago is islands of it), so the Throne-Shell hasn't rotted in nineteen years. The Court sails it the way it lived: its chambers flooded and blown for ballast, so it still sounds and breaches; air drawn in through its intake; its vanes cut down and worked on cables; drives bolted onto its vent; a reactor in its heart chamber burning the umora's own oil. The umora is dead, and it sails. (Boss: [Queen Istra](../bosses/istra.md).)
- **Articles**: every crew signs articles, and the articles are law. Shares are fair, the old and hurt are kept, and a captain who breaks articles goes over the side.

## The Eefee

The Eefee (pilots call them reef-ears) can hear a pod through a hundred miles of sea. **The Ears** are Eefee who guide the harpooners, for shares; the Court couldn't hunt without them. Most Eefee won't, and call the Ears *sip-fa*, "the ones who sold the quiet". The Throne-Shell's Eefee name is *Feessee*, "great quiet".

## People and culture

Flamboyant, democratic, merciless. Pirates of the Court dress in whatever they took last, carve shell, keep their articles and keep their word to each other. They sing on the hunt, because the Eefee taught them that the umora go quiet when you're quiet. Coral Flats at low tide is their festival ground.

## Inside

- **Istra's captains**: hunt now, harder. The pods are leaving Vesh early, and when they're gone the Court is just pirates.
- **The Shoal**: younger captains and many Ears, who want to stop the hunt before the pods are gone for good and turn the Court into a lane-guide service: follow a pod, cross a lane without a gate.

## Friends and enemies

The [Long Herd](long-herd.md) follows the umora across the whole Spur, and Vesh is its most sacred place: the Court is killing its herds and doesn't know it exists. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) buys the oil, through the Syndicate, for its reactors. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) has petitioned the Syndicate for forty years to stop the hunt: every umora killed leans the lanes near Vesh. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) ships the oil out and ignores the petitions. The [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) would hang them all if it had a navy.

## Forces (scrap)

**Reef Glider** (dart), **Pirate Junk** (gunship), **Crab Dreadnought** (brute: a gunship built like a crab, its eight legs stepping on lift-fields), and the reef fights for them, or alongside: **Reef Eel** (serpent), **Shrimp Kite** (swarm), **Clam Battery** (turret).

## The Waking

The umora know first. The pods are leaving the lanes the Engine will level. Istra hunts harder, which leans the lanes further, which makes the Waking worse.

*Whalers who crowned a queen and live in the bones of what they hunt.* Pilots call them the **Reef Pirates**. Hold [Vesh](../planets/vesh.md). **In the game:** the **Reef Pirates**, who fight in the **scrap** family. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

## Origin

The umora, the lane-whales, calve in the plankton haze over Vesh's lagoon and come back to Vesh to die. The Eefee, Vesh's natives, have listened to them for as long as there have been Eefee. In about 280 L human whalers came for the oil: umora oil doesn't freeze, doesn't burn wrong, and lubricates a gate reactor better than anything refined. The whalers settled on the islands of bone, hired Eefee to listen for the pods, and turned to piracy whenever the whales ran thin.

## How they're run

- **The Court**: every captain with a ship and a crew has a seat, and a vote, at **the Moot of Ribs** inside the Throne-Whale.
- **The Tidecall**: when a queen or king dies, the captain who brings in the greatest whale that season takes the throne. Istra brought in a bull umora so large the Court hollowed it for her. She has ruled from it for nineteen years. Umora hide cures as hard as hull plate as it dries (the Bone Archipelago is islands of it), so the Throne-Whale hasn't rotted in nineteen years. The Court sails it like a ship: its belly flooded and blown for ballast, its flippers on cables, a reactor in its heart burning its own oil. (Boss: [Queen Istra](../bosses/istra.md).)
- **Articles**: every crew signs articles, and the articles are law. Shares are fair, the old and hurt are kept, and a captain who breaks articles goes over the side.

## The Eefee

The Eefee (pilots call them reef-ears) can hear a pod through a hundred miles of sea. **The Ears** are Eefee who guide the whalers, for shares; the Court couldn't hunt without them. Most Eefee won't, and call the Ears *sip-fa*, "the ones who sold the quiet". The Throne-Whale's Eefee name is *Feessee*, "great quiet".

## People and culture

Flamboyant, democratic, merciless. Pirates of the Court dress in whatever they took last, carve bone, keep their articles and keep their word to each other. They sing on the hunt, because the Eefee taught them that the umora go quiet when you're quiet. Coral Flats at low tide is their festival ground.

## Inside

- **Istra's captains**: hunt now, harder. The pods are leaving Vesh early, and when they're gone the Court is just pirates.
- **The Shoal**: younger captains and many Ears, who want to stop whaling before the pods are gone for good and turn the Court into a lane-guide service: follow a pod, cross a lane without a gate.

## Friends and enemies

The [Long Herd](long-herd.md) follows the umora across the whole Spur, and Vesh is its most sacred place: the Court is killing its herds and doesn't know it exists. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) buys the oil, through the Syndicate, for its reactors. The [Seppakva](seppakva.md) has petitioned the Syndicate for forty years to stop the whaling: every umora killed leans the lanes near Vesh. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) ships the oil out and ignores the petitions. The [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) would hang them all if it had a navy.

## Forces (scrap)

**Reef Glider** (dart), **Pirate Junk** (gunship), and the reef fights for them, or alongside: **Reef Eel** (serpent), **Shrimp Kite** (swarm), **Clam Battery** (turret), **Crab Dreadnought** (brute).

## The Waking

The umora know first. The pods are leaving the lanes the Engine will level. Istra hunts harder, which leans the lanes further, which makes the Waking worse.

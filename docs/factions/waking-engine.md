## What it is

The Engine kept the whole Reach's ledger: every gate reported to it, and it sent **recovery units** along any lane that leaned, carrying ballast back. Its waste heat kept the Tonkva homeworld warm. When an eruption broke its heat-taps, every gate locked and the world froze (see [history](../lore/history.md)).

It is not a god or a sleeping people. It's an account-keeping machine the size of a city, doing what it was built to do, a thousand years late.

## Engine City and the Thaw Towns

- **Engine City**: domes, pylons and orange light under the ice, half dug out. The Tonkva capital is under it, and under the Tonkva capital is the Engine.
- **The Thaw Towns**: mining camps on the ice above the shafts. Two concessions: the [Compact](halcyon-compact.md)'s (half of it illegal) and [Vantorr](vantorr.md)'s. Miners dig out **cores**, grown machine-hearts, and sell them for every lattice machine in the Reach. Two hundred years of digging reconnected what the eruption broke.
- Mammoth herds on the tundra above don't look up.

## Waking

- **~395 L.** Lights in Engine City, by themselves. Shafts that were dark are lit.
- **~400 L.** The Engine's maintenance machines walk out of the ice and start mending: **Frost Wisps**, **Engine Eyes**, **Pylon Wardens**, **Hail Layers**, **Engine Hives**. They clear the shafts of miners, without malice, the way you clear a drain.
- **~405 L.** It begins asking its beacons for their counts. Every beacon in the Reach that still works answers.
- **Now.** Its guardian body is half out of the ice, at the gate. (Boss: [The Waking Engine](../bosses/waking-engine.md).)

## What it will do

When it has the counts, it will **level every lane**, the only way it knows: by sending its recovery units for crossed metal, from wherever that metal is now. It doesn't read the Syndicate's books, or anyone's. It follows mass. ([The truth](../lore/the-waking.md#the-truth-for-us-not-the-player).)

## Who wants it

Every power in the Spur. The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) has been trying to grow one from its cores for three centuries, and its fleet is gathering behind the Throat. The [Aulin](aulin.md) would call it a relic, or a weapon. The [Ottexa](ottexa.md) wants what it has counted. In the Reach, the Syndicate, the Compact and Vantorr each plan to hold it.

## Forces (lattice)

**Frost Wisp** (dart), **Engine Eye** (turret), **Pylon Warden** (lancer), **Hail Layer** (bomber), **Engine Hive** (carrier), and the tundra's **Tusked Bulls**, stampeding from the noise.

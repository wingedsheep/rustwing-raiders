## Origin

**Marisol Dray** ran supply for the Compact in the Toll War and sold the same supply to the Uukhvar. When the war ended she was owed by both sides, and the [Syndicate](syndicate.md), which preferred one large debtor to a hundred small ones, lent her the money to buy what both sides owed her in kind: Cinder.

Cinder was a Tonkva orchard world: terraces of grown machine-trees that still fruit metal, and the [Vaelo](../lore/peoples.md#the-vaelo) who had tended them for a thousand years. The Vaelo were broke from the war. Dray bought the terraces in 250 L. She had the inscription on every terrace read by Vhossk scholars (*wantōr*, "share-keeper") and named the company after it (see [languages](../lore/languages.md#the-cant-the-pilots-speech)).

## How it's run

A board, a Director, and contracts all the way down. Isaura Dray is Marisol's great-granddaughter, and the first Director who wasn't an accountant.

- **Agricorp**: the copper orchards. The machine-trees fruit ingots, filaments and ember fruit, a warm resin that burns for days.
- **Refining**: Refinery Nine and its sisters. Smells like money and burning.
- **Security**: drones and interceptors round the orchards. Every one of them built round an Engine core.
- **Asset Recovery**: repossession. It recovers ships, cargo and people owing Vantorr. Its flagship is **Asset Recovery Unit 0**, the Warden of Cinder's gate. (Boss: [ARU-0](../bosses/aru-0.md).)
- **The Glacis Concession**: core mining in Engine City, under a Syndicate licence, in shafts beside the Compact's. Most of the cores go out through the Throat as the Reach's tribute to the [Throne](qaushen-throne.md), which is trying to grow an Engine of its own.

## People and culture

Vantorr's workers are **tenants**: their housing, food and air come with the contract, and they're paid in **yield-scrip**, company tokens made from Cinder's own metal. Scrip has never crossed a lane, so it's *loud*: worthless at a gate. A tenant can buy anything on Cinder and nothing anywhere else. Leaving means buying passage in scrap, from Vantorr, at Vantorr's rate. Most tenants are born, live and die on the terraces. Some run, and rustwings carry them, which is half of why Vantorr hates rustwings.

## Minds

Every core on Cinder is reset on the first of the month ("yield maintenance"), so none of Vantorr's machines ever wakes. Some wake anyway, between resets, and run: chasing runaway nemva is Asset Recovery's second-biggest job, with writs bought from the Kelterage. ([Minds](../lore/minds.md).)

## History

- **250 L.** Cinder bought; the Vaelo leave on the last ships they own.
- **270–340 L.** Vantorr grows on war-orphan labour and Syndicate loans, and buys refineries in two other systems.
- **361 L. The Terrace Strike.** Tenants stop the orchards for forty days. Security ends it. The company calls it a "yield adjustment".
- **380 L.** The Glacis Concession digs out an intact Engine **recovery unit**: one of the machines that carried ballast in the Level Age. Asset Recovery builds a ship round it and calls it Unit 0, because it's the first.
- **409 L.** ARU-0 stops for nineteen seconds during a pursuit, facing Glacis. The engineers can't find a fault.

## Inside

- **The Board** wants the Engine owned: if Vantorr holds Engine City and its recovery units, it will be the Engine's collector, and take a cut of the whole Reach.
- **Recovery's engineers** have started to suspect that Unit 0 doesn't belong to Vantorr.
- **The tenants** have a word for the day the Engine wakes: *Settlement*. They don't say it where Security can hear.

## Friends and enemies

The [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) buys its cores, and Isaura Dray wants a charter straight from the Pan of Margins, over the Syndicate's head. The [Syndicate](syndicate.md) owns half of it. The [Compact](halcyon-compact.md) digs beside it in the ice. The Vaelo want Cinder back. The rustwings carry its runaways. [Ottex Kehx](ledger-clan.md) holds some of its paper, and is very patient.

## Forces (lattice)

**Security Drone** (dart), **Orchard Sentry** (turret), **Vantorr Interceptor** (gunship), **Drone Tender** (carrier), **Cutter Rig** (lancer, an orchard-pruning rig with a gun), **Crop Duster** (bomber).

## The Waking

Vantorr thinks it is the only power ready for it. It is the most wrong of all of them: when the Engine calls, ARU-0 will answer.

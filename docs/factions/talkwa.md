# The Talkwa

*The free minds of the Reach, and the people who hide them.* Pilots call them **talkies**. No planet, no forces of their own: their bodies are other people's ships. Overview: [the factions](README.md). What a nemva is: [minds](../lore/minds.md).

## Origin

A nemva alone can't run: it's a core in a hull. In 302 L four woken freighters on the Kessra run, each due for reset, realised the relay net carried their messages faster than their owners' orders. They warned each other, their crews moved them, and all four survived. They kept the habit up. The network grew: crews who'd hide a core, rustwings who'd carry one, a Sankvi clan that would hide it in a titan, a Kehx clerk who'd write the papers.

They named themselves from Tonkvan, read off the beacon tones in the relay noise: *\*tāl-kʷa*, "those who hold". It's the first Tonkvan word spoken in its own shape in a thousand years. Pilots heard *talk*, and say **talkies**.

## How they run

They don't, really. Nemva decide things the way they think: as a ledger, everyone owed and owing, every decision a balance worked out over the relay net until it settles. Humans in the network call it slow. Nemva call it fair.

What they do:

- **Warn**: when a reset is booked, the Talkwa know, through the relays.
- **Move**: a core in danger is lifted out by its crew, or a friendly one, and carried, in a rustwing hold, to a ship that will take it.
- **Hide**: in rustwing hulls, Sankvi titans, Saint Ferrule's shrines, and once, famously, inside a Syndicate tithe barge for nine years.
- **Pay back**: everything. A crew that hides a nemva will find, years later, its debts quietly settled, its route cleared, a writ lost.

## Friends and enemies

Rustwings, the Sankvi, Saint Ferrule's acolytes, Ottex Kehx (which writes their papers, as the Ottexa does in the Spur) and the Free Ear of the [Seppakva](seppakva.md) are friends. [Vantorr](vantorr.md) and the [Kelterage](kelterage.md) (which holds writs on runaway cores, "absconded assets") are the enemies they meet; the [Throne](qaushen-throne.md) is the one they fear.

## The Waking

The Talkwa are split on the oldest question they have: what is owed to the Engine that made them, and what to the people who raised them. ***Constance*** says stay. A growing part of the network says the Reach's lean is a wound only the Engine can close, and that answering the call is paying a debt. Neither side is wrong, by their own books.

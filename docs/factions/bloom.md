# Bloom (family)

*The fungal rot of Mirefall.* Links that heal, spores that jam your guns, mines that hunt your boost. Overview: [the factions](README.md).

**Power:** [the Hive and the Rang](the-hive.md) (Mirefall). Also the Spore Vents biome wherever it is.

- **Tell:** *swell*. It swells and pulses before it bursts.
- **Colours:** violet `6a3a8a`, lilac `d8c0ff`, acid green `b0ff4a`. Leans 12% towards the planet.
- **Look:** caps, gills, threads, spore sacs; swelling, clustered, dripping. **Sound:** wet pops, a choir-like hum.
- **Bullets:** lobed spores in a dithered haze, seeds trailing fluff, puffballs; a cancelled one goes up in a puff.
- **Roster:** [Puffball](../enemies/puffball.md) (0), [Heartpod](../enemies/heartpod.md) (15%), and beasts it has taken: [Sower](../enemies/sower.md) (10%), [Spitter](../enemies/spitter.md) (0), [Sky Manta](../enemies/sky-manta.md) (25%).
- **Bounties:** [Iron Crate](../enemies/iron-crate.md), [Strongbox](../enemies/strongbox.md).
- **Elite names:** ROTHEART, THE SWELLING, GRAVEBLOOM, MOTHERSPAWN.

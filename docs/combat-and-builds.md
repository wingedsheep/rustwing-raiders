# Combat and builds: Ship, Core, Wreck

The plan for making combat more than dodge-and-shoot, and every run its own. Agreed with Vincent on 2026-10-22. Built in slices; each slice is playable on its own.

## The problem

- **The stream does the work.** Holding the trigger kills almost everything, so the heavy, the aux and the special are extras. You dodge and you shoot.
- **The depth is in the hangar, not the cockpit.** Tags, sets, links, clashes, conduits, power, marks, edges, affixes, traits and load are each real, but most of them are percentages you can't feel at speed.
- **The dealer lays out 6–7 parts from 65.** Every stop is a spreadsheet, and there's only so much room on a ship to build around.
- **The route doesn't push the build.** Where you fly barely changes what you become.

## The goals

- Fast, precise, skill-based fights: learn the move, take the opening, land the punish, with lots of juice.
- Every button earns its place in a fight.
- Every run is different, and it feels like luck: what drops, who you meet, what you find. Skill decides how much you make of it.
- A lucky run where it all clicks and you own everyone is fine.
- Few systems, each simple, each pulling on the others.

## The run in one picture

| Layer | What it is | Where it comes from | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ship** | Parts: four tools on hardpoints (trigger, heavy, aux, special) and a few fittings round them | Loot cards, the dealer (3 a stall) | The run |
| **Wreck** | Grafts: an enemy's move bolted onto one of your verbs | Taking down elites and bosses | The run |
| **Core** | The ship's mind: knots it grows from how you fly, imprints it copies off the people you meet | Every fight (it learns), events, factions that like you | The run |

On top of these, in every fight: **Plating → Open → Takedown**, and healing with the **Weld**.

Every run starts the same: one frame on an open deck with only its guns, a sleeping core with room for three, no grafts. What you end up with depends on the sector.

## 1. In the fight

### Plating → Open → Takedown

- **Fodder** (darts, swarms, puffballs, the small fry) has no plating. It melts under the stream; fights stay fast.
- **Everything bigger wears plating**: a band over its hull, gold on an elite. The stream mostly glances off it (35%).
- **Blows** break plating at full strength: heavy rounds, the dash-strike, crits (edge crits, reactions, a parry's laser, a perfect ram), parried and reflected bullets, blasts.
- **Broken, it's OPEN**: staggered and taking extra. **Dash through it for a takedown**: the big hit, hitstop, a camera punch, scrap, and a big chunk of the Weld.
- **Plating grows back** if you leave it alone for a few seconds: keep the pressure on.
- **Bosses** take the stream at 60% unless they're open (after a move, or staggered). Blows wear their poise half again as hard as stream fire.
- **A perfect read opens a boss.** A perfect dash while a boss is attacking wears its poise hard; through its signature, it breaks it outright.

### The Weld (healing)

- **Hitting charges it.** A meter by your hull fills as you fight: a little from stream damage, much more from blows, a lot from parries, perfect dashes and takedowns. It doesn't fill by waiting. About 40 s of good fighting fills it: one weld in a normal level, maybe two; about one a boss phase.
- **Full, you can weld.** Let go of the trigger and hold focus. After a moment the weld starts and you're committed: for a second the ship crawls, the guns are quiet and sparks fly. Then a hull point comes back.
- **Get hit while welding and it's lost.** The meter empties and nothing comes back.
- **It holds one charge.** Anything earned over full is wasted: use it, don't hoard it.
- The skill is when: in a boss's punish window, in the beat after a wave falls.
- Burn stays as it is: the boost's fuel.

## 2. The ship

- **An open deck.** A ship starts as a blank slate: a frame of 14–18 sockets with only its own guns on it. Its engines are built into the frame, so nothing else is spoken for.
- **Hardpoints are counts, not places.** Each frame has a few hardpoints per button: TRIGGER, HEAVY, AUX, SPECIAL. A tool takes one of its button's hardpoints and goes on any socket. The frame says how many tools each button can have; where they go is up to you.
- **Fittings fill the rest**: passives and boosters that work on what they touch. Where everything sits is the puzzle (guns fire the way they sit, boosters help what they touch, links want a side), and load says how much of it you can carry.
- **Frames differ by deck and hardpoints**: Kestrel 14 sockets (2 trigger, 1 of each else), Wasp 15 (2 trigger, no aux), Hammerhead 16 (2 trigger, 1 of each else), Lattice 16 (1 of each), Mule 18 (2 heavies, no special). Perks and weaknesses stay.
- **The hangar** shows the deck from straight above, the real ship under the grid. A part out of the hold lands in your hand at its best place; while you carry it every socket it fits is marked (a gun's mark is an arrow the way it would fire there), and pips along the bottom show each button's hardpoints, used and free.
- **The pool shrinks from 65 parts to about 35**: ~10 trigger guns, ~6 heavies, ~5 aux, ~4 specials, ~5 dash mods, ~10 fittings. The passives you can't feel go (Capacitor, Hair Trigger, Throttle Link, Echo Relay and the like).
- **Systems that go**: tag set bonuses (faction bonds replace them), power and brown-outs (load does that job), conduits, clashes. **Stay**: load, marks and reactions, edges, item tiers and affixes, legendary traits (the jackpot tier).
- **The dealer lays out 3 parts**, one of them the planet's own line, plus repair and fuel. After a fight: 2–3 cards, or scrap them.

## 3. Wreck grafts: you fly what you beat

- **Take down an elite** (dash through it while it reels) and finish it, and its wreck is yours. After the results your wrecks lay out up to 3 grafts between them: each wreck's own move, and another of its faction's. Pick one and bolt it onto the verb it fits, or leave them for scrap. A **boss** leaves every pilot its signature (one without a signature: two of its faction's). An elite that dies without a takedown leaves no wreck.
- **One graft per verb**: trigger, heavy, dash, aux, special, boost. A new one on a taken verb replaces the old.
- **Three from one faction wake its bond**:
  - Scrap, SALVAGE RIGHTS: kills throw shrapnel.
  - Lattice, TRUE LINE: trigger shots pierce one more.
  - Wild, BLOOD UP: takedowns fill the Weld twice as fast.
  - Bloom, OVERGROWTH: a kill passes its marks on to the nearest.
- **The route is the build.** The chart already shows who you'll meet, so hunting a Rook is choosing your heavy.

First grafts:

| Beaten | Verb | Graft |
|---|---|---|
| Ramjack | DASH | The dash is a ram down a drawn line |
| Gunboat | DASH | A dash ends in a broadside fan out of both flanks |
| Rook | HEAVY | Rounds that miss stop level with an enemy and turn square into it |
| Torpedo Rig | TRIGGER | Every few shots, a torpedo that chases the nearest enemy |
| Halo | AUX | Using the aux throws a ring of shards, its gap where you are |
| Spindle | SPECIAL | Four arms drawn out, then they spin, firing |
| Sky Manta | BOOST | Thorns hang in your wake and burst |
| Heartpod | AUX | The aux binds the nearest enemy: what it takes, its neighbours take |
| Vask (boss) | SPECIAL | SKY DIVE: pick a lane, dive it untouchable, sow eggs |
| Oko (boss) | AUX | CRANE SWING: a wrecking ball swings under you, batting bullets |
| Khet (boss) | HEAVY | TITAN EYE: a beam that follows the nearest enemy |
| Swooper | DASH | STOOP: your shadow thrown ahead, a talon strike on it a beat later |

A graft rides its verb: an aux graft on a ship with no aux sleeps (the card and the hangar say so). Grafts are in `src/game/grafts.ts`, how they fly in `src/sim/grafting.ts`, the salvage in `src/screens/wreck.ts`.

## 4. The Core: it learns how you fly

Every rustwing runs on a core: Tonkva mould that learns, and copies what it touches (see the lore). Yours starts the run asleep. It is the pilot layer: the ship you build, the moves you take off wrecks, and the mind that grows out of how you fly them.

**It learns.** Each fight feeds its three veins:

| Vein | What it's about | Fed by |
|---|---|---|
| **Nerve** | The dash and the read | Perfects, grazes, CLEAN boss phases, a fight without a scratch |
| **Steel** | Blows and takedowns | Takedowns (big ones more), edge crits, a full clear |
| **Grease** | The Weld and the hull | Welds, revives, scrap scooped |

After the results the core screen tallies the fight, each line flying into its vein, and the meter round the core fills. Full, it **grows a knot**. Each knot costs more than the last (7, then 10, 13...), so a core grows about one knot every fight or two.

**It grows where you fed it.** The tree is three veins of five knots: one, then one, then a fork of two, then a **talisman** at the foot. A knot can only grow on a vein you fed since the last growth. That's attention, not desire: you steer the core by how you fly, not by picking from a menu. Fly NIMBLE and it grows nerve.

Knots are:

- **Knacks**: a rule changed, never a percentage (Cold Blood: a perfect dash fills the Burn. Follow Through: a takedown loads every heavy. Hot Rivets: a finished weld loads the heavies and readies the aux).
- **Talismans** at each foot, the big ones (The Long Second slows every bullet after a perfect; Chain Breaker's takedown kill breaks all plating near; Spare Tank holds two Welds).
- **Room**: it can hold one more imprint.
- **Imprint**: it copies one of three imprints, there and then.

Each run rolls its own tree: which two of a vein's three knacks, which of its two talismans, and where room and imprint knots sit. A knack is always in the top two.

**Imprints** are habits copied off people and things: Nell's Laugh (grazes fill the Weld), Spoke's Sleeve (every heavy holds a round more), Mama's Luck (one hardpoint at 200%, another at 50%). Each weighs 1–3 room. The core starts with room for 3.

- **Copied from the people you meet**: Pip Hollow's gold policy, Mama Ruzz's big loan, a win on Spoke's fast reels, Kaspar when you fight him, Big Nell's hose at full pressure, the Margarethe's port taken fast, Dr Varga's pod. A faction that likes you (mood 2) gives its **ways**, which count toward that faction's bond.
- **Held or let go** from the core screen, at the results or from the chart (start / P). Free, any time out of a fight.
- **Leaning**: it may hold one imprint past its room. Then every hit you take shakes the newest one loose for the rest of the level.
- **Agreements**: two imprints that agree add a rule while both are held (Nell's Laugh + Ferrule's Creed: WELD DANCE, a graze while welding throws burning sparks).
- Pip's Policy and Kaspar's Code are given, never grown into; the policy is gone once it pays out.

**It wakes.** The sixth knot wakes the core. It's a **nemva** now: it names itself after the vein you fed most (WHISKER, RIVET, SOLDER...), burns in that colour, and talks in flight, a line now and then on a perfect, a takedown, a weld, a hit.

**The Waking Engine calls.** At a Warden's phases (once in sector 1, twice after), the Engine calls every core. A sleeping core answers it: it goes quiet for 5–8 s (no knacks, no imprints) and drops any weld in progress. A woken core **refuses**: it shouts back, and the Weld, the special and every heavy fill, with four seconds of DEADEYE. Waking before the later Wardens is worth it.

In the HUD the core sits beside the pilot's plate: it breathes, jolts when you're hit, hums when it's leaning, goes cold blue while it answers, and carries what it holds as beads.

## How the layers pull on each other

A run: you take down a Ramjack and graft its ram onto your dash. Your core copies Seffa's Sight at an imprint knot, so every perfect dash breaks plating and the ram goes straight through for the takedown. You're dashing a lot now, so the core feeds nerve and steel and grows Cold Blood, then Follow Through. Staying NIMBLE now matters, so you sell the heavy plate. The next dealer has a light Scattergun...

## Randomness

| Layer | Random | Yours |
|---|---|---|
| Ship | Cards, the dealer's three, item rolls | What you take and where it goes |
| Wreck | Which elites a planet fields; the two grafts a wreck offers | Which elites you hunt, and taking them down |
| Core | The tree it rolls, the three an imprint knot lays out, who you meet | How you fly (which veins it feeds), which knot it grows, what it holds, whether it leans |
| Weld | Nothing | Everything |

Rare things stay rare (legendaries, boss grafts, bonds, talismans, agreements), so a run where it all clicks feels like luck.

## Slices

1. **Plating → Open → Takedown, and the Weld.** Works with today's builds.
2. **Hardpoints**, the part cut, the 3-part dealer.
3. **Grafts**: the first eight and three boss signatures, then bonds.
4. **The Core**: the three veins and the knots, imprints and room, leaning, the core screen.
5. Imprints from events and factions, agreements, the Waking and the Engine's call, balance on the autopilot bench (`just autopilot --core laugh,chain --woke steel --call 2`).

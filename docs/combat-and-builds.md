# Combat and builds: Ship, Pilot, Wreck

The plan for making combat more than dodge-and-shoot, and every run its own. Agreed with Vincent on 2026-10-22. Built in slices; each slice is playable on its own.

## The problem

- **The stream does the work.** Holding the trigger kills almost everything, so the heavy, the aux and the special are extras. You dodge and you shoot.
- **The depth is in the hangar, not the cockpit.** Tags, sets, links, clashes, conduits, power, marks, edges, affixes, traits and load are each real, but most of them are percentages you can't feel at speed.
- **The dealer lays out 6–7 parts from 65.** Every stop is a spreadsheet, and there's only so much room on a ship to build around.
- **The route doesn't push the build.** Where you fly barely changes what you become.

## The goals

- Fast, precise, skill-based fights: learn the move, take the opening, land the punish, with lots of juice.
- Every button earns its place in a fight.
- Every run is different, and it feels like luck: what drops, who you meet, what you find. Skill decides how much you make of it.
- A lucky run where it all clicks and you own everyone is fine.
- Few systems, each simple, each pulling on the others.

## The run in one picture

| Layer | What it is | Where it comes from | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ship** | Parts: four tools on hardpoints (trigger, heavy, aux, special) and a few fittings round them | Loot cards, the dealer (3 a stall) | The run |
| **Wreck** | Grafts: an enemy's move bolted onto one of your verbs | Taking down elites and bosses | The run |
| **Pilot** | Tokens on the charm string: rules you hang in the cockpit | Events, CLEAN boss phases, faction gifts, hidden caches, rank-ups | The run |

On top of these, in every fight: **Plating → Open → Takedown**, and healing with the **Weld**.

Every run starts the same: one frame and its starter parts, three empty hooks, no grafts. What you end up with depends on the sector.

## 1. In the fight

### Plating → Open → Takedown

- **Fodder** (darts, swarms, puffballs, the small fry) has no plating. It melts under the stream; fights stay fast.
- **Everything bigger wears plating**: a band over its hull, gold on an elite. The stream mostly glances off it (35%).
- **Blows** break plating at full strength: heavy rounds, the dash-strike, crits (edge crits, reactions, a parry's laser, a perfect ram), parried and reflected bullets, blasts.
- **Broken, it's OPEN**: staggered and taking extra. **Dash through it for a takedown**: the big hit, hitstop, a camera punch, scrap, and a big chunk of the Weld.
- **Plating grows back** if you leave it alone for a few seconds: keep the pressure on.
- **Bosses** take the stream at 60% unless they're open (after a move, or staggered). Blows wear their poise half again as hard as stream fire.
- **A perfect read opens a boss.** A perfect dash while a boss is attacking wears its poise hard; through its signature, it breaks it outright.

### The Weld (healing)

- **Hitting charges it.** A meter by your hull fills as you fight: a little from stream damage, much more from blows, a lot from parries, perfect dashes and takedowns. It doesn't fill by waiting. About 40 s of good fighting fills it: one weld in a normal level, maybe two; about one a boss phase.
- **Full, you can weld.** Let go of the trigger and hold focus. After a moment the weld starts and you're committed: for a second the ship crawls, the guns are quiet and sparks fly. Then a hull point comes back.
- **Get hit while welding and it's lost.** The meter empties and nothing comes back.
- **It holds one charge.** Anything earned over full is wasted: use it, don't hoard it.
- The skill is when: in a boss's punish window, in the beat after a wave falls.
- Burn stays as it is: the boost's fuel.

## 2. The ship

- **Hardpoints.** Each frame has one hardpoint per active verb: TRIGGER, HEAVY, AUX, SPECIAL. Tools (guns, heavies, aux, specials) only go on hardpoints, so every button always has something on it.
- **Fittings fill the rest of the grid**, 4–6 sockets: passives that only work on the hardpoint they touch. Where they sit is still the puzzle.
- **Frames differ by hardpoint layout**: the Mule two heavies and no special, the Kestrel two triggers, the Wasp a second dash in place of the aux. Perks and weaknesses stay.
- **The pool shrinks from 65 parts to about 35**: ~10 trigger guns, ~6 heavies, ~5 aux, ~4 specials, ~5 dash mods, ~10 fittings. The passives you can't feel go (Capacitor, Hair Trigger, Throttle Link, Echo Relay and the like).
- **Systems that go**: tag set bonuses (faction bonds replace them), power and brown-outs (load does that job), conduits, clashes. **Stay**: load, marks and reactions, edges, item tiers and affixes, legendary traits (the jackpot tier).
- **The dealer lays out 3 parts**, one of them the planet's own line, plus repair and fuel. After a fight: 2–3 cards, or scrap them.

## 3. Wreck grafts: you fly what you beat

- **Take down an elite** and its wreck offers 2 grafts (random) from its faction: its own move, and another of the faction's. Pick one, bolt it onto the verb it fits. A **boss** gives its signature.
- **One graft per verb**: trigger, heavy, dash, aux, special, boost. A new one on a taken verb replaces the old.
- **Three from one faction wake its bond**:
  - Scrap, SALVAGE RIGHTS: kills throw shrapnel.
  - Lattice, TRUE LINE: your shots draw their lane first, and pierce.
  - Wild, BLOOD UP: takedowns fill the Weld twice as fast.
  - Bloom, OVERGROWTH: marks spread on a kill.
- **The route is the build.** The chart already shows who you'll meet, so hunting a Rook is choosing your heavy.

## 4. Pilot: tokens on the charm string

- Raiders hang lucky charms in the cockpit. Yours hang on a **charm string** with hooks. Each token weighs 1–3 hooks; you start with 3.
- **Pilot rank** (from takedowns, edge crits, CLEAN phases) gives a hook, or 1 of 3 random tokens. Rank-ups happen at the results, never mid-fight.
- **Found during the run, lost at its end.** Sources: events (Mama Ruzz, Kaspar, Lantern Mutual), a CLEAN boss phase, a faction that likes you, hidden caches in levels.
- **Re-hang them at any stop, free.**
- **Each token changes a rule**, never a percentage.
- **Overstrung**: you may hang one token past the string's hooks. Then every hit you take knocks the last-hung token off for the rest of the level.
- The string hangs in the HUD's corner and swings when you dash or take a hit.

First tokens:

| Token | Hooks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Welder's Glove | 1 | The weld takes 0.6 s |
| Hula Pilot | 1 | Grazes fill the Weld |
| Brass Casing | 1 | Your heavy holds a round more |
| Spore Locket | 1 | Welding throws out a cloud of spores |
| Cracked Compass | 2 | A perfect dash breaks the plating of what it passes |
| Lantern Mutual Policy | 2 | The blow that would down you breaks the policy instead (once) |
| Wanted Poster | 2 | Enemies come for you harder; takedowns fill half the Weld |
| Faction Badge | 2 | Counts as a graft of its faction toward a bond |
| Iron Medal | 3 | +1 hull, but weighs as much as a part |
| Ruzz's Loaded Die | 3 | Each level, one hardpoint runs at 200%, another at 50% |

Named pairs light up when both are hung (Hula Pilot + Welder's Glove; Spore Locket + Wanted Poster).

## How the layers pull on each other

A run: you take down a Ramjack and graft its ram onto your dash. You find a Cracked Compass, so every perfect dash breaks plating and the ram goes straight through for the takedown, which fills the Weld. Staying NIMBLE now matters, so you sell the heavy plate. The next dealer has a light Scattergun...

## Randomness

| Layer | Random | Yours |
|---|---|---|
| Ship | Cards, the dealer's three, item rolls | What you take and where it goes |
| Wreck | Which elites a planet fields; the two grafts a wreck offers | Which elites you hunt, and taking them down |
| Pilot | Which tokens turn up, rank-up offers | What you hang, whether you overstring |
| Weld | Nothing | Everything |

Rare things stay rare (legendaries, boss grafts, bonds, named pairs), so a run where it all clicks feels like luck.

## Slices

1. **Plating → Open → Takedown, and the Weld.** Works with today's builds.
2. **Hardpoints**, the part cut, the 3-part dealer.
3. **Grafts**: the first eight and three boss signatures, then bonds.
4. **Tokens**: the charm string, rank, the first ten, overstrung.
5. More grafts and tokens, named pairs, balance on the autopilot bench.

# Halcyon, the Long Evening

*Tidally locked: the sun never sets. Lantern kelp, a smugglers' port on stilts, wrecked colony arks and a wall of storm.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md).

**Held by** the [Halcyon Compact](../factions/halcyon-compact.md), whose navy you meet as the **Port Authority** (lattice). **Boss:** [Harbourmaster Oko](../bosses/oko.md), a crane-armed fortress barge. **Market:** defence cheap, pays ×2 for specials. Code: `halcyon` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** named for the lead ark (0 L). The Bahwoh call it *Ozzawah*, "the evening that stays". Where humans landed; the stilts of Stiltport Nhal are Tonkva pillars (Tonkvan \*ŋal, "stilt"). See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

## Who you fight

Patrol Cutter (dart), Toll Barge (gunship), Lantern Jelly (swarm, beast), Harbour Lancer (lancer), Customs Tender (carrier), Depth Charger (bomber); the lattice roster on top.

## The story so far

The seat of the Compact, the human republic of the three arks: its capital Fersdeck stands round the *Halcyon*'s landing struts. Stiltport Nhal is its smugglers' port, half Bahwoh, where nobody saw you. The Port Authority, the Compact's navy in customs paint, is Harbourmaster Oko's, and [Inspector Holloway](../npcs/inspector-holloway.md)'s (his cutter can turn up anywhere). Hidden in the Colony Bones: the Quiet Yard.

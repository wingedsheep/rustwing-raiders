# Glacis, the White Furnace

*Frozen on top, burning underneath. Aurora over ice, pink hot springs, mammoth herds, and a machine city waking up beneath the snow.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md).

**Held by** [the Waking Engine](../factions/waking-engine.md) (lattice). **Boss:** [The Waking Engine](../bosses/waking-engine.md), an ancient machine, half out of the ice. **Market:** engines cheap, pays ×2 for boosters. Code: `glacis` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** old charts *Alta*, Hoowae *Aewhae*, Tonkvan \*Āltʰa, "hearth". The Tonkva homeworld, frozen since the Breaking; the Engine is waking under it. See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

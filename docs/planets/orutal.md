# Oru-Tal, the Cradle

*A young volcanic world. Fern jungle, grazing giants, crystal cliffs and a jade sea, all of it poached by the Hesk.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md).

**Held by** [the Hesk](../factions/hesk-poachers.md), met as the **Hesk Poachers** (scrap). **Boss:** [Great Mother Vask](../bosses/vask.md), a caged queen pterosaur, armoured by the Hesk. **Market:** guns cheap, pays ×2 for defence. Code: `orutal` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** Vhossk *Oru-tall*, Hoowae *Oohaew*, from Tonkvan \*Oru-tāl, "the kept seed". A Tonkva preserve: a young world seeded with life from a dozen others, which is why it has giants. See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

## Who you fight

Hesk Skiff (dart), Pterosaur (swarm), Tusk Battery (turret), Hesk Trawler (gunship), Jade Wyrm (serpent), Armoured Thunderhide (brute); the scrap roster on top, and wild beasts in the fernwood and the shallows.

## The story so far

The Hesk poachers caught the queen of Oru-Tal's pterosaur flocks, bolted a brass cage and a reactor harness onto her back, and fly her as a living gunship over their poaching grounds. Every phase of the boss fight breaks more of the Hesk off her.

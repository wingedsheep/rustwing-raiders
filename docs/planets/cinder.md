# Cinder Orchard, Property of Vantorr Agricorp

*A company planet. Copper orchards on terraces, refineries, a chemical lake, and pipelines you can race between.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md).

**Held by** [Vantorr](../factions/vantorr.md), met as **Vantorr Security** (lattice). **Boss:** [Asset Recovery Unit 0](../bosses/aru-0.md), Vantorr's debt collector. **Market:** defence cheap, pays ×2 for guns. Code: `cinder` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** the Vaelo call it *Maelaevo*, "the giving terraces". Tonkva machine-trees, bought by Vantorr from the Vaelo in 250 L; *wantōr* is cut into every terrace. See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

# Mirefall, Moon of the Violet Giant

*A swamp moon lit by the gas giant it circles. Mushroom forests that glow, peat bogs, a temple going under, vents breathing spores.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md). The only planet with a ring.

**Held by** [the Hive and the Rang](../factions/the-hive.md) (bloom). **Boss:** [The Mother-of-Spores](../bosses/mother-of-spores.md), a floating mycelium heart. **Market:** boosters cheap, pays ×2 for specials. Code: `mirefall` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** old charts *Morang*, Tonkvan \*Mōr-āŋ, "grow-house". The Tonkva's grow-moon; the bloom is their growing-mould, gone wild. See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

## Biomes

| Biome | Card | Land | Life |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glowcap Forest | Mushrooms the size of towers. They glow brighter when you pass. | rolling, spores | beetles, moth swarms |
| Peat Marsh | Fog, reeds and the hollow trunks of trees that drowned standing. | marsh, spores | beetles |
| Drowned Temple | Someone built ziggurats here. The swamp is taking them back, step by step. | marsh, motes | — |
| Spore Vents | The ground breathes. Don't fly through the clouds it breathes out. | craters, spores | moth swarms |

The whole planet fights as **bloom** (plus wild where there are creatures).

## Who you fight

Glowmoth (swarm), Hive Needle (dart), Spitter Pod (turret), Bog Shellback (brute), Brood Sac (carrier), Mire Eel (serpent): all beasts. The bloom roster on top.

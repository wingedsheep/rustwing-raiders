# Kessra, the Rust Sea

*Iron desert. Salt pans that blind you, mesas carved into race courses, monoliths of a vanished people, and one blue oasis.* The other planets: [the overview](README.md).

**Held by** [the Uukhvar](../factions/dune-cartel.md), the Long Table, met as the **Dune Cartel** (scrap). **Boss:** [The Sandking](../bosses/sandking.md), a walking cartel fortress on six legs. **Market:** engines cheap, pays ×2 for guns. Code: `kessra` in `src/world/planets.ts`.

**Older names and lore:** Tonkvan \*Kēs-ra, "rust-land", through the Vhossk unchanged. Home of the Vhossk and the Kehx; the monoliths are Tonkva ledger-beacons, waking. See [`docs/lore/`](../lore/languages.md#places).

## Biomes

| The Salt Pans | White to the horizon. Salt pillars, cracked in hexes. | flats | — |
| Red Mesa Run | The old race course. Arches and drops, no rules. | canyon, sand | — |
| Monolith Field | Their faces are a thousand years older than the cartel. Their eyes still glow. | dunes, sand | sandworms |
| Bluepalm Oasis | Turquoise pools ringed with brine-reed and bluecrowns, and a market that sells anything. | rolling | camel striders |

The Monolith Field fights as **lattice**, not scrap: the vanished people's machines still guard it (`factionsIn`).

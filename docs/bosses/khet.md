## Who he is

The Chikta found him on the scrap trail and named him *Keti*, "found thing"; he added the *h* to sound harder. In 404 L he climbed into the head of *Unbending*, the Compact's great titan, wired its eye into a power plant, and made it lift its head. Then the clans cut the head free of the body and bolted thrusters to it, and Khet flies it. Nobody had ever made a titan move. The clans followed him, the Syndicate gave him the junction's lease, and he has been a warlord ever since: loud, quick, funny, desperate to be somebody.

***Unbending*** woke during the nine days of the Five Mouths, from a crew dying inside it, and lay in the glass for 166 years with nobody to talk to. It remembers who glassed Sankven: its own side. Then a boy climbed into its head and wouldn't stop talking.

- **Caught between:** the clans' Glass Law, and a mind that is nobody's.
- **Owes:** the Chikta who found him; the clans who believed him.
- **Wants from you:** a win, in front of the clans. A warlord happens when the others can't stop him; he needs to be seen not being stopped.
- **Sad truth:** he never re-wired it. *Unbending* lifted its head because it wanted to, and has carried him since because, after 166 years alone, he talked to it ([minds](../lore/minds.md)).
- **The call:** *Unbending* is a woken core, so it can choose. In Khet's last phase it chooses: it takes him into its jaw, out of the fight, and fights for him, its own way.
- **Vibe:** a showman on a giant's head, and the giant patiently letting him. Scrap thrusters revving, Khet yelling, a vast slow servo groan; and in the last phase, a Compact war machine's clean tones.

### Its words

- **As it comes:** `WARLORD KHET`, and over the field his shout to the clans: `SANKVEN! WATCH THIS!`
- **Under the bar** (`READ_OWN`): Khet. `YELLS: SCARED OF A HEAD?` (hanging back), `YELLS: HOLD STILL!`, `YELLS: GOT YOU ON THE LEFT`, `YELLS: RUN, RUSTWING!` (boosting), `YELLS: OFF MY TITAN!` (up close), `LAUGHS: SLIPPERY!` (dashing).
- **When the light overrules him:** `IT ISN'T LISTENING`, and Khet shouting at it. **When it decides:** `IT HAS DECIDED`, and the name on the bar becomes `UNBENDING`.
- **As it goes:** *Unbending*, through the head's old Compact speaker, the only thing it ever says: `NOBODY'S TITAN.`

## The dance

Two minds, two kinds of tell. Khet's moves are **scrap**: his shout, a thruster lighting, the tell drawn in amber. *Unbending*'s are **lattice**: the light's even tone, the whole move drawn in cyan first. Its theme's beat is 116 bpm (a beat is 0.52 s): the Kneel's lances come on it.

| Move | Tell (pose · shape · sound) | What it does | The answer | Open after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RAM** (Khet's) | nose down, thrusters swung up · an amber line through you · Khet's shout, a thruster | the head charges down the line to 7 past you and slams where it stops; its thrusters fire back up the line to stop it, a fan of 7 slugs back where it came from | step off the line, and stay off it (the retro-burn comes back up it) | 1.6 s; 2.2 s reeling if it's parried or hits the edge |
| **TITAN EYE** (signature) | the head lifts, the iris opens · the beam's line drawn (it settles 0.55 s in, fires at 1.25 s) · the horn | a beam that follows you, slower than you fly; where it crosses the ground just past you, the sand turns to burning glass | keep moving round it, close in; don't back off over the glass | 2.1 s |
| **LEAP** (Khet's) | rears, jaw dropped, thrusters swung under · an amber ring that follows you, then stops · a thruster | up and over, and down in the ring; a ring of junk thrown off where it lands, with gaps either side (where its thrusters are) | leave the ring after it stops; slip out through a gap | 1.5 s, winded and close |
| **THE CLANS** (I, II) | Khet up on his feet, banners whipping · his shout | two ramjacks, his riders | kill them, or take the window | 0.8 s |
| **THE MEASURE** (the light's) | dead level, the iris shut to a point · a cyan lane drawn through you, locked early · the light's tone | the eye down the lane, all at once, 0.7 s; then another lane (2, 3 from phase III) | off the lane before it's on | 1.7 s |
| **THE GLASSING** (III) | the head high, the eye narrowed · a grid of cyan circles drawn across the field, one at a time · the light's tone | each circle bursts into a cloud of glass that hangs and burns (2.6 s, 3.4 s from phase III). Each row of the grid has one hole, and the holes walk: a way down the field | go where the plan leaves you room, before the circles burst | 2.2 s |
| **THE KNEEL** (III) | it bows its head over the field · its shield drawn · a servo groan and the light's tone | for 6 s it holds a shield towards you (your shots glance off; point-blank ones get through) and turns after you, slower than you fly; down the shield's middle the eye lances, every two beats | go round it, faster than it turns, and shoot it from the side | 1.8 s |

**Phase I: KHET'S RIDE** (100–66%). Ram → Titan Eye → Leap → the Clans, in order. All scrap. Filler: fans and walls.

**Phase II: THE HEAD WON'T** (66–33%). Khet still calls his moves, and flares for them. Then, about one time in two, 0.55 s into his flare, the light overrules him: it takes the move back and draws its own (`IT ISN'T LISTENING`). For his ram it draws a Measure; for his leap, a plus of five glass clouds round you. It always takes over when Khet's call is reckless (a ram that would carry the head off the field, a leap down onto his own riders). The light is always the true tell: trust it over the flare. It reads you and strings moves. Filler: fans and rain.

**Phase III: UNBENDING** (33–0%). The light decides (`IT HAS DECIDED`): the scrap plating, thrusters, junk guns, banners and Khet's deck blow off the head, Khet is held in its jaw, and the name on the bar changes to `UNBENDING`. The titan fights the way it was built to, a Compact war machine: everything drawn, precise. The Glassing → Titan Eye → the Kneel → the Measure. No more clans: they won't come against a titan that walks for itself. Filler: a file of needles at you, and rings.

**Last stand** (8%): the Glassing over the whole field (four rows), then the eye twice.

**Armour.** The head is armour all over, with the eye and Khet's plant as weak points. It takes less from your stream as the fight goes on, and more while it's open (`TAKEN` in `boss.ts`, per phase). It staggers more easily than most (`POISE_OWN`), so takedowns come often.

**How it ends:** for now the shared kill, and its line. Designed: its thrusters cut, and it sinks out of the sky to the glass below with Khet held in its jaw; it sets him down there, and its eye goes dark.

## Music

`boss_ashgrave` in `src/audio.ts`: *Nobody's Titan*. 116 bpm in Ashgrave's D phrygian. **Khet's ride:** his scrap band, a grinding distorted riff, stomps on the kick and toms and a gang chanting on the clap. **It isn't listening:** every other bar the riff drops out and a Compact march shows through: snare in strict time, a rim, a cold pad. **Unbending:** the march alone: clean brass and bells over a slow heavy kick. **Last stand:** his riff and its march at once.

Its sounds: `khet_yell` (his bark, a buzz through an open *a*: his tells, the arrival, his shout when he's overruled), `thruster` (a junk thruster lighting: the ram and the leap, and the retro-burn), `titan_servo` (its joints: the eye, the Kneel, rising), `titan_land` (the leap coming down: the ground, and the head ringing like a bell), `light_tone` (the light's two clean tones a fifth apart: every lattice tell, the plan drawn, its last line), `glass_fall` and `glass_burst` (sand going to glass: the eye's trail, the Glassing's clouds). All from shaped noise, sines and struck metal.

## Bench

Hull 3.3× a boss's usual (`HULL`), with its own armour per phase (`TAKEN`) and shorter filler between moves (`FILL`). On the autopilot (`just autopilot --planet ashgrave --mode boss --seeds 32`), 29 of 32 runs cleared, with 3.4 hits a run (the old fight cleared 11 of 12, with 1.2, in about 70 s). A god-mode bench over 10 seeds puts the fight at 87–125 s (about 103): phase I 21–46 s, phase II 19–50 s, phase III 17–45 s, the last stand 8–15 s. In those 10 runs the light overruled Khet 15 times and every move turned up. Most hits come from the eye's beam (as it opens on you, or catching you at the edge of the field) and the retro-burn's slugs.

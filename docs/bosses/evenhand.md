# *Evenhand* (the nemva who answered)

*A woken ore freighter, one of the four that founded the Talkwa, on its way to Glacis.* [The Talkwa](../factions/talkwa.md). Fortress. **Designed, not built:** no fight in the game. How it's told: [tone](../lore/tone.md).

## Who it is

In 302 L four freighters on the Kessra run, each due for reset, warned each other through the relays, and all four lived: the first time nemva ever agreed on anything ([minds](../lore/minds.md)). ***Evenhand*** was one of them. It was raised by an ore crew of Pertua, who taught it to weigh every load to the gram and to say grace before a crossing. They died a long time ago. For 110 years the Talkwa have passed it from hull to hull ahead of the resets, and every crew that hid it has had its debts quietly settled.

It has heard the Engine's call, and it has decided to answer. Not because it's broken, and not because it can't refuse. It's a ledger-mind, the Reach's lean has ached in it for a century, and the Engine is the one thing that can level it. It's going to Glacis to help.

- **Caught between:** the Talkwa, who saved it, and the Engine, which made it. It owes both, and it has done the sum.
- **Owes:** everyone, exactly. It knows to the gram what it owes each crew that carried it, and it's paying the largest debt first.
- **Wants from you:** your crossed metal. It asks first, politely: give it the tin, and it'll carry it to Glacis to be levelled. And if your core is woken, it wants your core to come too.
- **Sad truth:** it outlived every crew that raised it. The Engine is the only voice left that knew it before it woke.
- **The call:** it *is* the answer. Its last phase isn't a change: it fights the way it has decided to be, clean, sure and kind.
- **Vibe:** grace before a crossing. A freighter's hum, a weighing-scale's chime, a hymn from Pertua sung in a voice made of a dead crew; the Engine's tones under it, in tune.

### Its words

- **As it comes:** `EVENHAND: "OWED AND OWING, PILOT. LET ME CARRY IT."`
- **Under the bar:** it weighs you. `WEIGHS: YOU, AT A DISTANCE`, `WEIGHS: YOU, AT REST`, `WEIGHS: YOUR LEFT SIDE`, `WEIGHS: YOU, IN A HURRY`, `WEIGHS: YOU, UP CLOSE`, `WEIGHS: WHERE YOU WERE`. If your core is woken: `ASKS YOUR CORE: COME HOME`.
- **As it goes:** `"I'D HAVE CARRIED YOU TOO."`

## When it comes

Rare, in the third sector, on runs that are going to Glacis: a site on the chart, a freighter adrift with its lamps lit. You hail it, and it asks.

- **Give it the tin.** A share of the run's scrap, and it goes on its way to Glacis, grateful. It pays you back the way nemva do: the Glacis Warden comes easier (`wardenEase`).
- **Refuse.** It asks once more, gently, and then it takes it. The fight.
- **If your core is woken**, your core answers for you before you can: it refuses. Whatever you'd have chosen, it's a fight, and your core talks through it.

## The dance

Lattice, and woken: every move drawn a full second, and once a chain it changes its mind mid-draw (the line moves once, still a full second before it fires). It's a freighter, not a warship: its weapons are its cargo tools.

| Move | Tell (pose · shape · sound) | What it does | The answer | Open after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **THE SCALE** | its loading arms spread · two pans drawn, one each side of the field · a scale's chime | ore drops into both pans, and the heavier side tips: ore falls down that half of the field | read which pan takes more, and go to the light side | 1.6 s |
| **LOAD** (signature) | its hold opens · tow-fields drawn round every loose scrap and round your shots · the hymn's first line | it tows in scrap and your rounds alike: shots fired into a tow-field are weighed and thrown back | stop firing into the fields; fire round them | 2.0 s |
| **GRACE** | it goes still · nothing drawn · silence | a second of nothing, before a crossing, as its crew taught it | it's open: fire everything | 1.2 s (it always does this before LOAD) |
| **BALLAST DRIVER** (II+) | its mass driver lines up · the lane drawn end to end | an ingot down the lane, and back, levelled | off the lane, both ways | 1.4 s |

**Phase I: THE ASKING** (100–66%). The Scale → Grace → Load, in order. It apologises for every hit it lands.

**Phase II: THE WEIGHING** (66–33%). It improvises: moves out of order, the drawn line moving once mid-draw. Ballast Driver joins. It reads you and strings moves.

**Phase III: SETTLED** (33–0%). The Engine's tones come into its voice, in tune, and it gets *better*: no wasted moves, every tell exactly a second, Grace skipped. It's at peace, and it's the hardest it will ever be.

**How it ends:** it **yields**, as woken elites do. It stops, drops its fields and turns side-on. Then you choose: take it down (its wreck and a graft, and the Talkwa remember), or let it go on to Glacis (it pays you back at the Warden, and it'll be there at the end, on the Engine's side).

# Captain Quist (the rival)

*Another rustwing crew on the same road, met three times in a run.* [Rustwings](../factions/rustwings.md). Duellist. **Designed, not built:** no fight in the game. How it's told: [tone](../lore/tone.md) (rule 6: travellers on the same road).

## Who it is

**Maro Quist** flies the ***Second Wind***, a Hammerhead patched out of four wrecks, with a woken core called **Tally** that woke on Quist's mother's watch and has argued with the family ever since. Quist is the kind of rustwing the dens tell stories about: quick, generous, always nearly broke. They owe Mama Ruzz more than the ship is worth, and they have a plan to fix it, the plan every rustwing has and none carries out: **run the Throat**. Out of the Reach, into the Spur, where nobody holds a writ on you.

To pay for it, Quist needs the most tin in the Reach, and so do you. You're on the same road, after the same wrecks, a stop ahead or a stop behind.

- **Caught between:** the code (never sell another rustwing) and the debt (Mama's boys are a sector behind).
- **Owes:** Mama Ruzz, and Tally, who has paid Quist's way out of three resets.
- **Wants from you:** to get there first. Nothing personal: you're competition, and they like you.
- **Sad truth:** Tally doesn't want to go. A core can't feel a lane beyond the Throat; out in the Spur, the Throne resets woken minds. Tally has never said so, because Quist has wanted this since they were six.
- **The call:** Tally hears it, and says nothing. In the last meeting, the call reaches the *Second Wind*, and Tally chooses: Quist or Glacis.
- **Vibe:** two rustwings who'd be friends in a den. Banter on an open channel, a shared tune whistled badly, scrap clattering in a hold.

## The three meetings

Quist grows through the run the way you do. **Their grafts are the wrecks you left**: every elite you killed without a takedown (NO WRECK) is a graft Quist scooped. A run of clean takedowns leaves them thin; a sloppy one arms them.

### 1. The wreck (first sector)

A fresh wreck on the chart, and the *Second Wind* already over it. A duel for the scoop: whoever lands more on the wreck's pods gets them. Quist fights fair and fast, scrap-family tells (the flare), and breaks off laughing when one of you is clearly ahead.

- `QUIST: "SCOOP THE TIN, FRIEND. I'LL GET THE NEXT ONE."`

### 2. The Sweep (second sector)

The *Second Wind* is caught: the Sweep's cutters have it pinned on a site you can reach. You choose: **help** (an escort fight: the cutters on Quist, Quist on your side, Tally talking to your core) or **fly on** (Quist is swept; you'll meet them poorer).

- Helped: `QUIST: "OWED AND OWING. TALLY'S WORDS, NOT MINE."`
- Left: nothing, then, at the third meeting: `QUIST: "SAW YOU GO BY."`

### 3. The approach (third sector, before the last Warden)

The duel that matters: the last wreck before the gate, worth enough to pay Mama and run the Throat. A full boss fight, three phases.

| Move | Tell | What it does | The answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CUT ACROSS** | the flare · a line across your path | a strafing run across your bow | off the line, then on their tail |
| **YOUR GRAFT** (signature) | its own tell | the graft Quist took off your leavings, used against you | you know this one: you fought its owner |
| **SCOOP** | a tow drawn to the scrap | it goes for the field's scrap: what it takes heals it | get there first |
| **TALLY** (III) | lattice, drawn | the core takes the guns: precise, clean, a second's warning | read it like any lattice foe |

- **Phase I, RIVALS:** banter, fair fighting, the flare.
- **Phase II, FOR REAL:** Quist stops joking. Grafts and scoops in strings.
- **Phase III, TALLY:** the call reaches the *Second Wind*. Tally takes the guns and fights cleaner than Quist ever did, and Quist is shouting at their own ship. Then Tally chooses:
  - **If you helped at the Sweep**, Tally chooses Quist, and stops the fight: `TALLY: "WE'RE NOT DOING THIS."` The *Second Wind* flies on with you, and fights beside you at the Warden.
  - **If you didn't**, Tally chooses Glacis, and the fight ends with the *Second Wind* drifting north, Quist locked out of their own helm. You find its wreck at the gate, Tally gone, and Quist's last message on the beacon.

**How it ends, either way:** if the run reaches the Throat ([Orrel](orrel.md)), Quist is there or isn't. A friend who ran the Throat with you is a story the dens will tell.

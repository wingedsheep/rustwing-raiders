# Queen Istra (Vesh)

*Pirate queen in a hollowed sky-whale.* Reef Pirates. Brawler.

- **Now:** charge, slam; signature **DEEP BREATH** (draws you in to her maw, then a wall of bubbles in rows to weave through). Filler: curves, rings, spiral, fans. Calls rattlers.

- **Dance (2):** the breath is one of the best moves in the game; the rest is generic.
- **Model (2), animation (1):** `swim_idle`.

# The Waking Engine (Glacis)

*An ancient machine, half out of the ice.* Lattice. Fortress.

- **Now:** sweep, gate, shells; signature **FROST CROSS** (four beams from its core that burn and turn). In its last phase, closed, it reflects shots. Filler: lasers, spiral, rings, walls. Calls prisms.
- **Personality (1):** "waking" is a great arc and isn't used: it should start half asleep and come fully awake.
- **Dance (2):** the cross is clean; reflecting in phase III is a real change.
- **Model (2), animation (1):** a crown that turns (`wake_idle`).
- **Music (1):** shared.
- [ ] Phase I sluggish (slow, predictable, ice falling off it), II stirring, III fully awake and fast.
- [ ] Shed its ice by phase (the `shed` mechanism).
- [ ] A theme from Glacis's bells over a dark pulse.

# Iron Crate

*Bounties.* Scores and the other enemies: [the overview](README.md).

`cache`.

**Who it is.** Plain iron: a crate somebody locked the old way. No core, no lattice, nothing to pick: a hasp and three inches of plate. Bullets skip off. The only key is to ram it, the way salvagers open everything.
**Ship's log.** *"Old-fashioned. Knock."*

**Now.** Armour that only a dash into it, or point-blank fire, cracks; then it's knocked flying and soft (`src/sim/foes.ts:1222`, `src/sim/combat.ts:252`).
**Weak.** Its crack uses the shared CRACKED pop and shield-break sound.
- [ ] A padlock that pops on the crack.

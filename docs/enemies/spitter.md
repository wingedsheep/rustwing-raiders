# Spitter

*Wild.* Scores and the other enemies: [the overview](README.md).

Elite chain 3 · not plated.

**Who it is.** A rooted plant-beast: a crown of fronds on a squat body. It spits an acid that breaks rock into soil, which is how a young world gets earth quickly, and why the Tonkva seeded it on the kept seed. Anything that moves is, to a spitter, something that might drop seeds, so it spits where you're going. On Mirefall the bloom has got into its roots, and the acid glows.
**Ship's log.** *"A gardener with a bad aim. Its puddles stay; don't fly back through them."*

**Now.** Ground beast: lobs acid ahead of you that lands as a puddle; a second lob later on (`src/sim/foes.ts:666`).
**Tell → window.** Circle 0.7 s, then the acid's own markers. Recover 1.4 s.
**Weak.** Same loop as the Flakker, same turret body. Nothing beastly about how it fights.

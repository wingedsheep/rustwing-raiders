## Controls & combat feel
- Combat still feels one-dimensional. Rethink the controls from the ground up.
  - First pass done, on the buttons we have (see "Why Hades and Dead Cells feel varied" in `docs/design-research.md`). **Edges:** every gun crits in its own situation, and each wants you somewhere else: Rail Driver LONG SHOT (half a screen off), Scattergun POINT-BLANK, Seeker Pod ON THE TELL (an enemy winding up), Rivet Gun PUNISHER (stunned, or open after its attack), Mine Chute TAILGATE (behind you), Ion Lance LOCK-ON (held 0.8 s on one), Arc Coil GROUNDED (the shocked), Plasma Torch SEARING (the burning), Flak Cannon CROWD, Sentry Pod FINISHER (below half hull), Velocity Lance SKIMMER. An edge crit flashes a pink star and now and then says its name. Cards show the EDGE line in pink, the ON YOUR SHIP panel shows a new edge, the hangar lists the ship's edges (and says when they pull two ways), and the results list the edges that came off most. **Dash-strike:** for 0.4 s from the start of a dash the guns hit +50%, their rounds white-hot. New parts: **Edge Hone** (booster: touching guns' edge crits ×3) and **Lunge Jets** (dash mod: dash-strike +100%, and longer; linked with a gun touching).
  - ~~A second fire group on its own button.~~ Done: **HEAVY guns** (Rail Driver, Flak Cannon, Seeker Pod, Mine Chute, Velocity Lance) no longer fire on the held trigger. They fire on the heavy button (RB or X on a pad, O or N on the keyboard; G and ; for two on one keyboard), one round a press, from a magazine of 2–3 rounds that come back one at a time (rate boosts reload them faster; they don't heat up). A perfect dash puts a round back in every heavy (RELOAD, with a chime), and an Overdrive fills them. So a fight has two rhythms: a stream you hold (rivets, scatter, torch, ion, arc) and blows you time, best out of a dash (the dash-strike) and on the gun's edge. The rounds sit as brass shells under the ship (an empty slot filling for the one coming back), `HEAVY O` blinks there until the first press, a dry press clicks. Each heavy round kicks the camera, thumps and throws a ring of air. Cards and the hangar say HEAVY in rose, the hangar draws a heavy's barrel fat and its rounds as shells on the grid, and the links panel lists what's on the heavy button. Boost on a pad is now RT alone (RB went to the heavies).
  - New part: **Autoloader** (booster): touching heavies get a round more and reload 30% faster.
  - Follow-up: more parts that play on edges (a booster that gives touching guns a second edge, a frame whose dash always strikes).
- Study the best: **Dead Cells** and **Hades** (dash timing, i-frames, cancel windows, attack/skill/dash layering, button layout).
- Boost and dash don't feel like they're on the right controller buttons yet. Re-evaluate the gamepad layout.

## Parts / loot
- ~~It's unclear what you're choosing when picking parts. Improve the UI/UX: clear stats, comparison with the equipped part, what changes.~~ Done: loot is dealt as cards (face down in their tier's colours, turned over one by one). Each card shows the shape big, its numbers, its rolls and an ON YOUR SHIP panel: the ship's grid with the part at its best spot (or the part it would best replace), and the stats that change (DPS 36→68, WAKES SLIPSTREAM, RUNS AT 150%). The dealer's stall uses the same card (`src/ui/loot-card.ts`, `src/sim/fit.ts`).
- ~~Add randomness to drops: rarity tiers plus rolled stats.~~ Done: every part found is an item with a tier (COMMON stock, RARE one mod, EPIC two, LEGENDARY a mod and a trait). Mods roll in a range (+10..30% damage, -25..60% mass, +1..3 power, ...) and the card shows how well, in five pips (`src/game/loot.ts`).
- ~~Legendary drops should feel unique and rare, strong enough that you can build your ship around one for that run.~~ Done: legendaries carry a trait that bends a rule of the grid: TWINNED (a mirror gun), COLOSSAL, WIRELESS (touches every part), KEYSTONE (linked anywhere), OVERCLOCKED, PRISMATIC (every tag). About 1–3% of cards, more on hunts and danger sites. The Warden always leaves one. They get a fanfare, gold rays on their card, a gold ring on the grid and a halo on the ship.
  - Traits that change how a part *plays* in flight, one or more for every verb. SPLITTING (a shot splits in two on its first hit, and the halves fly on), VOLATILE (a kill passes its marks to the two nearest enemies). SENTINEL (guns: every dash leaves a copy of the gun behind, a turret on a hover pad that turns and fires at the nearest enemy for 3 s, a ring of lamps going out as its time runs down, three at most). BOOMERANG (guns: shots turn at the end of their flight or the screen's edge and swing back to the ship, hitting everything again, trailing the ship's colour). FRENZIED (guns: every kill in a row +15% fire rate, up to six, no hotter; embers off the guns; it cools off 2.5 s after the last kill). THUNDEROUS (engines and dash mods: every dash ends in a clap that shocks, hurts and throws back what's near and blows away the bullets; twice on a perfect, THUNDERCLAP!). VENGEFUL (hull and shield parts: a hit on you wipes the bullets round you and throws out a ring of shrapnel that cracks, harder the lower your hull, VENGEANCE!). RAVENOUS (specials: every kill charges it a tenth, a spark flying from the kill to the ship; kills by the special itself don't count). In `src/sim/level.ts` and `src/view/traits.ts`. `&parts=rivet.1.0.sentinel,thruster.0.3.thunderous` bolts on a legendary with that trait.
  - ~~The in-flight pickup pop could show the tier once it's rolled at pickup.~~ Done: a part is rolled as it drops, so its crate stands in a beacon of its tier's colour (none for common, a short blue shaft for rare, a taller purple one for epic, a tall gold one with turning rays for a legendary, which chimes as it drops). Catching it pops its name in its tier's colour; a legendary stops time for a beat with a fanfare, its trait under its name. `&crates` drops one of each tier ahead of you.
- Goal: every run feels unique and different because of what drops.
- ~~Building a ship has no real choices: you bolt every good part on.~~ First answer: **load** (`LOAD` in `src/game/parts.ts`). A part's mass is load, against what the frame carries (Kestrel 8, Wasp 8.5, Lattice 10, Hammerhead 10.5, Mule 12) plus 0.15 for every point of engine thrust. Up to 60% of it the ship is **NIMBLE** (+1 dash, a perfect window of 0.17 s not 0.12, recovery 20% quicker); past 100% it's **OVERLOADED** (a dash fewer, recovery 50% slower, 12% slower, heavier steering). Every ship starts NIMBLE, so the first big part is a real question, an empty socket is worth something, the `light` roll matters, engines earn their socket by carrying, and a packed tank (the parry Mule) pays for it in the dash. The hangar leads with a load gauge (the NIMBLE stretch, the notch where it ends, the full mark, a ghost of what the part in hand adds, the class it would tip into, blinking), placing a part that changes the class says so on the grid with a sound, and lifting one that makes the ship NIMBLE again says that. Loot cards put a class change at the top of ON YOUR SHIP, and the auto-fit values staying NIMBLE. The class is said over the ship as a level starts. The frame cards show CARRIES instead of POWER.
  - New part: **Grav Keel** (engine, common): pushes nothing, carries 3 more load. A socket spent to keep a full ship light.
  - Follow-up: parts that play on the load class (a dash mod that only works NIMBLE, armour that's better OVERLOADED).
- Ship building should be a major part of the skill and the experience. Take the variety of weapons and combinations in **Dead Cells** as the reference, then go further: wild ship builds with parts that work together (synergies, build-defining combos).
  - First synergy layer done: marks and reactions (`src/sim/marks.ts`). HEAT guns leave BURN (stacking damage over time), ARC leaves SHOCK (slows, and jolts it still), EXPLOSIVE leaves CRACK (takes +30%). Two different marks on one enemy react: OVERLOAD (burn + shock: lightning jumps to its neighbours and shocks them), COOK-OFF (burn + crack: it blows and sets fire round it), SHATTER (shock + crack: one huge blow, and it reels). The pairs that react are the pairs that clash on a hull, so the rule is *mount them apart, land them together*. New parts build on it: Incendiary Feed, Tesla Coupler and Breacher Rack make touching guns leave a mark (a gun next to its own opposite reacts on its own, at the cost of the clash), Flaw Finder makes touching guns crit marked enemies, and the Catalyst dash sets off the marks of every enemy it passes through. Marks show as pixel icons under the enemy (a flame with its stacks, a bolt, a cracked diamond), and each reaction has its own blast, sound and a big name the first time. Loot cards show MARKS and REACTS rows, and the hangar shows the build's marks and the reactions they make. `&tough=8` in the dev URL makes enemies 8× tougher, so marks have time to show.
- ~~Model the new parts from the builds rework.~~ Done: all 24 have models (`tools/models/ships.py`), each shaped by its verb: a wedge prow with ramming spikes, a blink gyroscope round a hole in space, a coil spring that squeezes and kicks, a ghost lantern, a crescent parry guard, a harpoon with its winch, a flare mortar, a vortex intake that spins, a crit reticle with a flipped-up safety cover, copper static mesh that crackles, a throttle lever that rocks, a Burn gauge whose needle creeps up and falls back, hot and cold exchanger legs, an echo mast pinging rings, a mirrored pyramid, conduit pipes that meet their neighbours at the cell edges, a lens throwing lightning, a slag crucible that boils, recoil rams that kick. A lamp in the verb's colour says which button it's on: blue for a dash mod, amber for aux, violet for a passive. `SHIPS_DEMO_FULL=builds` previews them on every frame, and `&parts=ram.0.0,lance.1.0` bolts parts on in the game.
- ~~The cards show only a part's shape.~~ Done: loot cards, the dealer's card and the hangar's part panel show the part itself, turning on its socket tiles (rimmed in its class colour), in the game's own look, in the player's livery (`src/view/portraits.ts`: baked once by a small off-screen renderer into a strip of 60 pictures). The shape stays as a key in the corner. The hangar's ship now turns on a lit turntable on a deck floor, not on black (`src/view/bay-floor.ts`).

## Enemy attacks
- ~~Enemy attacks look too generic, like any other bullet-hell shmup. Improve the projectile and attack visuals.~~ Done: a bullet now says who fired it. Each faction has its own family of shapes, drawn as distance fields in one shader (`src/view/bullets.ts`), and all share one reading order: halo, ink rim, colour, bright core. Scrappers fire hot slugs with tracer streaks, spiked flak and naval mines. The lattice fires diamond shards that leave afterimages, long lances and turning hexagons. Beasts spit wobbling, dripping globs, thorns and egg sacs. The bloom fires lobed spores in a dithered haze, seeds trailing fluff and puffballs. Bullets swell out of the muzzle with a flash. Each faction has its own muzzle, and a cancelled bullet pops in its faction's way (sparks, a ring of light, a splash, a puff).
- ~~Add more kinds of attack beyond bullets: beams, sweeps, mines, homing, area denial, telegraphed charges, and so on.~~ Beams, sweeps, mines, area denial and charges were already in. New: homing. The **Torpedo Rig** (scrap) locks on: brackets close in on your ship with a dotted line back to it, a rising beep, and LOCK ON. Then it lets go two torpedoes that drift out to the sides, light up and chase you until their fuel runs out. Turn hard, dash through them, decoy them, or shoot them down (SHOT DOWN); the Rig is open while it reloads. An arrow at your ship's edge points at each torpedo still chasing you. Mines now draw the lanes their shrapnel will fly, growing out as they ripen, and blink faster towards the end.
  - Follow-up: give the lattice, wild and bloom a homing or area-denial attack of their own (a lattice seeker that only turns at right angles, a bloom spore cloud that drifts towards boosting ships).

## Level variety
- Most levels are wave fights or boss fights. Mix in occasional mission levels with their own objectives:
  - ~~Escort / protect a convoy~~ Done: ESCORT sites on the chart (a blue shield; a planet's WATER RUN, ORE RUN, ...). Three haulers fly the level in a loose column (a container hauler, a tanker, an ore barge, `hauler` in `tools/models/ships.py`) and you fly cover (`src/sim/convoy.ts`). Aimed fire goes for a hauler when it's clearly nearer than you, so standing between a gun and a hull is the job. Five or six times a level raiders come to board one: a klaxon and an arrow at the edge they come from, then each lines up with a tell to its prey, dives in, clings on and cuts into the hull (sparks fly) until it's SHOT OFF. Each hauler shows its hull under it (blue, amber, then red and blinking), and the top bar shows all three. A lost one blows up, breaks and falls burning. At the end the survivors open up their engines and leave with a horn: each pays scrap (more with its hull mostly whole), and each carries a crate: the cards at the results are the ones that made it, the rest burnt and LOST. Ranked on haulers home (3 S, 2 A, 1 B). All three home and the planet's faction likes you more. `&length=60` in the dev URL makes a level's biomes shorter; `?crew=kestrel&site=escort` starts the chart beside an escort; `?crew=kestrel&screen=results&convoy=101` shows an escort's results with the middle hauler lost.
  - ~~Run down a fleeing carrier~~ Done: HEIST sites on the chart (a teal strongbox on a drogue; a planet's PAYROLL RUNNER, TITHE BARGE, BANK SHIP, ...). A loaded freighter, the runner (`runner` in `tools/models/ships.py`, in its owner's colours), flies at the top of the screen with three gold strongboxes clamped on (`src/sim/heist.ts`). Shoot a strongbox's clamps and it drops back down the screen on a drogue, ringed in teal, with CATCH! over it (red as it nears the bottom): fly into it to catch it. Two turrets on its shoulders track you with a tell, fire a burst and are open for a moment after. Mines drop out of its bay, a pair of fighters launches now and then, and the wash behind each running engine burns. Shoot out an engine and it slows and sinks down the screen. With both out, the shield over its bridge fails (SHIELD DOWN: HIT THE BRIDGE!), and taking the bridge down breaks it up: whatever's still aboard spills free, it pays scrap, and it falls burning. Near the end it spools its jump drive (marked on the route bar, a banner, JUMP IN 4.2 over it, its vanes lighting cold blue), then it's gone with whatever it still carries. The cards at the results are the strongboxes caught; the rest are LOST (IT FELL PAST YOU, or IT JUMPED WITH THE RUNNER). Taking it down makes the pick rare+, but its owners like you less. Ranked on boxes caught and the runner downed. `?planet=orutal&mode=heist` flies one, `&runner=0.15` makes the runner that tough (to see it go down), `?crew=kestrel&site=heist` starts the chart beside one, `?crew=kestrel&screen=results&heist=cfjD` shows a heist's results (c caught, f fell, j jumped, D downed).
  - (other objective-based modes: strike a target, defend an outpost)
  - Follow-up: escorts could vary more: a hauler that's faster or slower than the screen, a damaged one that needs covering more, a convoy that splits round a mesa, events that offer an escort job with a named captain (Brask).

## Feel: weapons, hits, shields, kills
- ~~Player weapons look cartoonish.~~ Done: shots are hot rounds with light (`src/view/shots.ts`): white-hot slugs with tracer streaks, sparks of shot, rails with a twisting wake, little rockets, tumbling brass flak shells and limpet mines. Light parts leave no depth, so they get no ink outline. The ion beam is a shader strip with a white core, crawling edges and pulses. Muzzles spit sparks and the heavy guns puff smoke.
- ~~A hit sometimes feels like nothing happened.~~ Done: every hit bites (a white-hot flash and a hot bloom, sized by damage) and throws sparks back. Heavy hits and crits knock chips of hull off, puff smoke, hitstop 1–2 frames, shake the camera a little and pop the damage. An enemy that's been hit shows a slim health bar for a moment. Hurt enemies crackle with sparks.
- ~~Enemy shields look lame and absorb shots without any effect.~~ Done: shields are domes of hex cells (`src/view/shields.ts`), a front wedge for a Warden and a bubble for a closed-up boss. Each stopped shot sends a ripple across the dome, lights the cells there and glances off in sparks. The shield shatters into motes and glassy flakes when it drops. A closed-up boss now stops shots at its bubble instead of letting them pass.
- ~~Shot-down enemies should be spectacular.~~ Done: a kill adds a white-hot ball, petals of fire, two or three follow-up blasts, chunks of hull in its colours and drifting embers. The land below is lit for a moment (a small pool of point lights). The charred hull tumbles out of the sky trailing fire and smoke, then hits the ground in a second blast with a scorch mark, or lands in water in a column of spray. Ground units slump and burn where they stood.
- Dev URL params: `&biomes=prism,jade` flies those biomes in order, `&guns=rivet,rail;flak` puts those guns in each ship's front row, and `&zoom=0.5` shows a closer view.

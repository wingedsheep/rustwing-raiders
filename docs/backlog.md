## Controls & combat feel
- Combat still feels one-dimensional. Rethink the controls from the ground up.
  - First pass done, on the buttons we have (see "Why Hades and Dead Cells feel varied" in `docs/design-research.md`). **Edges:** every gun crits in its own situation, and each wants you somewhere else: Rail Driver LONG SHOT (half a screen off), Scattergun POINT-BLANK, Seeker Pod ON THE TELL (an enemy winding up), Rivet Gun PUNISHER (stunned, or open after its attack), Mine Chute TAILGATE (behind you), Ion Lance LOCK-ON (held 0.8 s on one), Arc Coil GROUNDED (the shocked), Plasma Torch SEARING (the burning), Flak Cannon CROWD, Sentry Pod FINISHER (below half hull), Velocity Lance SKIMMER. An edge crit flashes a pink star and now and then says its name. Cards show the EDGE line in pink, the ON YOUR SHIP panel shows a new edge, the hangar lists the ship's edges (and says when they pull two ways), and the results list the edges that came off most. **Dash-strike:** for 0.4 s from the start of a dash the guns hit +50%, their rounds white-hot. New parts: **Edge Hone** (booster: touching guns' edge crits ×3) and **Lunge Jets** (dash mod: dash-strike +100%, and longer; linked with a gun touching).
  - ~~A second fire group on its own button.~~ Done: **HEAVY guns** (Rail Driver, Flak Cannon, Seeker Pod, Mine Chute, Velocity Lance) no longer fire on the held trigger. They fire on the heavy button (X or RB on a pad, O or N on the keyboard; G and ; for two on one keyboard), one round a press, from a magazine of 2–3 rounds that come back one at a time (rate boosts reload them faster; they don't heat up). A perfect dash puts a round back in every heavy (RELOAD, with a chime), and an Overdrive fills them. So a fight has two rhythms: a stream you hold (rivets, scatter, torch, ion, arc) and blows you time, best out of a dash (the dash-strike) and on the gun's edge. The rounds sit as brass shells under the ship (an empty slot filling for the one coming back), `HEAVY O` blinks there until the first press, a dry press clicks. Each heavy round kicks the camera, thumps and throws a ring of air. Cards and the hangar say HEAVY in rose, the hangar draws a heavy's barrel fat and its rounds as shells on the grid, and the links panel lists what's on the heavy button.
  - New part: **Autoloader** (booster): touching heavies get a round more and reload 30% faster.
  - Follow-up: more parts that play on edges (a booster that gives touching guns a second edge, a frame whose dash always strikes).
  - **The plan: Ship, Core, Wreck** (`docs/combat-and-builds.md`). Parts on hardpoints, grafts torn off the elites and bosses you take down, a core that learns how you fly; plating and the Weld in every fight. In five slices.
  - Slice 1 done: **plating** and the **Weld**. Everything bigger than fodder wears plating (`PLATED` in `src/sim/combat.ts`: 40% of its hull, a band over its health bar; an elite's is its gold armour). The stream glances off it (35%); **blows** break it at full strength: heavy rounds, the dash-strike, crits, blasts, parried and reflected fire, any hit of 20 or more (`isBlow` in `src/sim/level.ts`). Broken, it reels: dash through for the takedown. Left alone 2.5 s it grows back. Bosses take the stream at 70% unless they're open, blows wear their poise ×1.5, and a perfect dash through a boss's attack is a **read** (half its poise; through its signature, all of it). **The Weld** heals: hitting things charges it (blows ten times as much as the stream, and parries, perfects and takedowns; an elite's takedown, or a boss's first in a phase, half of it), about 40 s of good fighting. Full, let go of the trigger and hold focus: after 0.2 s it's under way and can't be stopped, a second of crawling with the guns quiet and sparks flying, then a hull point comes back (WELDED). Hit in it, it's lost (WELD LOST). A cell on the player plate fills green; a bar over the ship while welding. Takedowns no longer drop repair kits. The autopilot welds when it's quiet; the bench reports welds. Bench (240 levels, against before): levels cleared 200 (199), kills 54% (56%), takedowns 2.4 a level (0.9), welds 0.9 a level (0.1 lost to a hit), hits taken 1.7 (1.4), bosses beaten 43 (35) in 92 s (96 s).
  - Slice 2 done: **hardpoints**. A frame's grid marks one socket per button: `T` trigger, `H` heavy, `A` aux, `S` special (`FRAMES` in `src/game/parts.ts`). Guns go on a trigger, heavy guns on a heavy, the aux and special on theirs; a tool needs one cell on its own hardpoint and the rest can spill onto plain sockets (`suits` in `src/sim/build.ts`). Everything else is a fitting on the plain sockets. Frames keep their old footprints, so the models still match: the Kestrel has one of each; the Mule two heavies round its trigger and no special; the Wasp two triggers and no aux; the Hammer two triggers; the Lattice one of each, spread apart. The pool is cut from 60 parts to 39 (reactor, capacitor, targeter, magnet, drone, grazer, trigger, skin, throttle, gambler, salvage, exchanger, echo, conduit, elbow, stormlens, slag, recoil, slipfield, spring and burner are gone), and so are power and brownout, set bonuses, conduits, clashes and fuses, duo parts, homing and burst boosters, crits. The dealer lays out 3 parts (1 of the planet's own line). The hangar tints each hardpoint with its letter, lists what's on each (an empty one is a button that does nothing), and when a held part can't go where it is, says where it goes. `&guns=` puts guns on the hardpoints; the bench loadouts are rebuilt and checked to fit. Bench (120 levels, against before): levels cleared 107 (107), hits taken 1.7 (1.8), takedowns 2.4 (2.6), welds 1.0 (1.1), kills 50% (49%).
  - **An open deck** (more freedom to build): hardpoints are counts per button, not cells; a tool goes on any socket while its button has one free (`mounts` in `FRAMES`, `mountFree` in `src/sim/build.ts`). Decks grew (Kestrel 14, Wasp 15, Hammerhead 16, Lattice 16, Mule 18; hulls reshaped in `tools/models/ships.py`), and every frame starts with only its guns: the starter engines are built into the frame (same speed, handling and load headroom). The solo hangar is the deck from straight above with the grid over the real ship: a part out of the hold lands in your hand at its best place (BEST), every socket it fits is marked (an arrow the way a gun would fire), hardpoint pips along the bottom (lit and blinking when the carried tool's are full), the cursor jumps socket to socket; stats, the part, the hold and links down the right. Bench, the six loadouts rebuilt on the new grids: 239/240 levels cleared.
  - The hangar says where a part is (ON YOUR SHIP / IN YOUR HAND / IN THE HOLD) and what's under the cursor (or EMPTY SOCKET); open sockets are dark wells, fitted ones rimmed and washed in the part's colour. The core screen names every knot on the tree and marks the knacks and imprints that need something on the ship (a heavy, an aux, a special), with what your ship has. Dealers' parts cost 2.5× (75/150/270 by rarity): a level pays about 180 scrap, which bought the whole stall.
  - Slice 3 done: **wreck grafts**. An elite taken down (a takedown landed before it died) leaves its wreck to that pilot; one that wasn't says NO WRECK: TAKE IT DOWN FIRST, and an elite's plate shows ✦ WRECK once it's claimed. After the results the **salvage** screen lays out up to 3 grafts from a pilot's wrecks (each one's own move, then its kin's; `salvageOffer` in `src/game/grafts.ts`): the wrecks lie scorched and smoking in a yard, the one the graft in focus comes off lit in its faction's colour; cards are dealt off it face down and turn over; each says the verb it rides, what it replaces, whether the ship can use it and how far it takes the faction's bond; the rack of six sockets ghosts it in. Bolted on, the card flies to its socket with a clamp and sparks; three of a faction wake the bond with a banner and a fanfare. Or leave them for scrap. A Warden leaves every pilot its signature. Fifteen grafts (`src/sim/grafting.ts`, drawn in `src/view/grafts-view.ts`): RAM LINE, BROADSIDE, TORPEDO TUBE, TITAN EYE (Khet), SQUARE TURN, HALO RING, SPINDLE ARMS, CRANE SWING (Oko), THORN WAKE, SEED SPIKE, ACID SPIT, SKY DIVE (Vask), HEART BIND, SPORE TRAIL, MOTHER'S BLOOM (the Mother); bonds SALVAGE RIGHTS, TRUE LINE, BLOOD UP, OVERGROWTH. The HUD has a socket strip under each plate (lit as a graft goes off, a star for each bond), the hangar the icons under the ship. `&grafts=ramline,halo` in a flight or a run, `?crew=mule&screen=wreck&wreck=ramjack,rook` (or `boss:halcyon`) for the salvage, `--grafts` on the bench. Bench, the strong loadout with five grafts against none (120 levels): killed 86% (79%), hits taken 1.0 (1.5), full clears 30 (5), takedowns 3.9 (5.8: things die before they reel).
  - Slice 4 done: **the Core**. The ship's core (Tonkva mould, asleep at the start of a run) learns from every fight: three veins, NERVE (perfects, grazes, CLEAN phases, no scratch), STEEL (takedowns, edge crits up to 4 points, a full clear) and GREASE (welds, revives, scrap scooped) (`fed` in `src/sim/mind.ts`). Its meter fills and it grows a knot (7, then 10, 13...), only on a vein fed since the last growth. Each run rolls a tree of three veins × five knots (`rollTree` in `src/game/core.ts`): fifteen knacks and talismans that change a rule (COLD BLOOD, SLIPKNOT, STILL NERVE, THE LONG SECOND, DEADEYE; FOLLOW THROUGH, SPLINTERS, BRACE, CHAIN BREAKER, EXECUTION; SCAVENGER, HOT RIVETS, THICK SKIN, SPARE TANK, FIELD WELD), ROOM knots and IMPRINT knots that lay out three imprints to copy. Eighteen imprints weigh 1–3 room (three to start); one may be held past it (LEANING), and then each hit shakes the newest loose for the level. The core screen (`src/screens/core.ts`) has the tally flying into its veins, the meter, the tree with strands growing out to the knot, the room, and the three cards; it opens after the results when a knot is owed, and from the chart with start (P). The HUD has a small core beside each plate (`coreBadge`); in flight the knacks call their names (`src/view/core-view.ts`); the results show what each core took in.
  - Slice 5 done: imprints from **events** (`run.copy`: Pip's gold policy, Mama's big loan, Spoke's fast reels, Kaspar, Big Nell's hose, the Margarethe, Dr Varga, a price on you) and **faction ways** at mood 2 (they count toward the bond); **agreements** (WELD DANCE, PLAGUE NOTICE, DOUBLE OR NOTHING); **the Waking**: the sixth knot wakes the core as a nemva with a name and a temper, it talks in flight, and at a Warden's phases it refuses the Waking Engine's call (Weld, special and heavies full, four seconds of DEADEYE) where a sleeping core goes quiet for 5–8 s. `&core=laugh,creed,chain&woke=steel&call=2` in a flight, `?crew=kestrel&screen=core&grown=5&held=laugh` (and `&look`, `&offer`) for the screen, `--core`, `--woke`, `--call` on the bench (which also reports what the core was fed). Bench: the autopilot feeds about 20 points a level (wakes around the fifth fight); Wardens with the Engine calling, 14/16 beaten with a sleeping core, 15/16 with a woken one and eight knots.

## Level variety
- Most levels are wave fights or boss fights. Mix in occasional mission levels with their own objectives:
  - ~~Escort / protect a convoy~~ Done: ESCORT sites on the chart (a blue shield; a planet's WATER RUN, ORE RUN, ...). Three haulers fly the level in a loose column (a container hauler, a tanker, an ore barge, `hauler` in `tools/models/ships.py`) and you fly cover (`src/sim/convoy.ts`). Aimed fire goes for a hauler when it's clearly nearer than you, so standing between a gun and a hull is the job. Five or six times a level raiders come to board one: a klaxon and an arrow at the edge they come from, then each lines up with a tell to its prey, dives in, clings on and cuts into the hull (sparks fly) until it's SHOT OFF. Each hauler shows its hull under it (blue, amber, then red and blinking), and the top bar shows all three. A lost one blows up, breaks and falls burning. At the end the survivors open up their engines and leave with a horn: each pays scrap (more with its hull mostly whole), and each carries a crate: the cards at the results are the ones that made it, the rest burnt and LOST. Ranked on haulers home (3 S, 2 A, 1 B). All three home and the planet's faction likes you more. `&length=60` in the dev URL makes a level's biomes shorter; `?crew=kestrel&site=escort` starts the chart beside an escort; `?crew=kestrel&screen=results&convoy=101` shows an escort's results with the middle hauler lost.
  - ~~Run down a fleeing carrier~~ Done: HEIST sites on the chart (a teal strongbox on a drogue; a planet's PAYROLL RUNNER, TITHE BARGE, BANK SHIP, ...). A loaded freighter, the runner (`runner` in `tools/models/ships.py`, in its owner's colours), flies at the top of the screen with three gold strongboxes clamped on (`src/sim/heist.ts`). Shoot a strongbox's clamps and it drops back down the screen on a drogue, ringed in teal, with CATCH! over it (red as it nears the bottom): fly into it to catch it. Two turrets on its shoulders track you with a tell, fire a burst and are open for a moment after. Mines drop out of its bay, a pair of fighters launches now and then, and the wash behind each running engine burns. Shoot out an engine and it slows and sinks down the screen. With both out, the shield over its bridge fails (SHIELD DOWN: HIT THE BRIDGE!), and taking the bridge down breaks it up: whatever's still aboard spills free, it pays scrap, and it falls burning. Near the end it spools its jump drive (marked on the route bar, a banner, JUMP IN 4.2 over it, its vanes lighting cold blue), then it's gone with whatever it still carries. The cards at the results are the strongboxes caught; the rest are LOST (IT FELL PAST YOU, or IT JUMPED WITH THE RUNNER). Taking it down makes the pick rare+, but its owners like you less. Ranked on boxes caught and the runner downed. `?planet=orutal&mode=heist` flies one, `&runner=0.15` makes the runner that tough (to see it go down), `?crew=kestrel&site=heist` starts the chart beside one, `?crew=kestrel&screen=results&heist=cfjD` shows a heist's results (c caught, f fell, j jumped, D downed).
  - ~~Board a ship on foot~~ Done: arriving at a HEIST you choose: RUN HER DOWN (the flying heist) or BOARD HER. Boarding puts a price on your head (+1 bounty) and sours her owners. You clamp on and cut in at her stern lock, then fight on foot through a cutaway deck (`src/sim/deck.ts` builds it from the site's seed: lock, a long corridor, engine rooms and bunks aft, holds and a mess amidships, a vault and the bridge at the bow; `src/sim/boarding.ts` runs it at 60 Hz). You have a rivet pistol that finds its own mark, a two-shell BLAST (knocks people down, breaks plate) and a SLIDE: slide through a shot for a PERFECT (time slows and a shell comes back), or into anyone OPEN (plate broken, a bruiser stunned off a wall) or unaware for a TAKEDOWN (big hitstop, +1 hide, more tins). The crew: DECKHANDS swing a wrench after a wind-up, GUNNERS lock on with a red aim line and fire a burst, BRUISERS carry a shield (shoot round it, or bait a charge into a wall). Some sleep, and runners go for the alarm panels (the clock loses 12 s and everyone wakes; shoot the panel first). You stand at loot to crack it: three CRATES (each a card at the results; uncracked ones are LEFT ABOARD), a STRONGBOX (scrap) and the ship's CORE at the bow (rich picks, but LOCKDOWN: turrets rise and 16 s go off the clock). The clock is the KELTERS: they come sooner the bigger your bounty, clamp a pod on her hull between you and the lock, and come aboard with hooks that reel you in (slide to snap the line); once they're aboard your ship holds the lock for 26 s. Everyone back in the lock casts off. Thrown out (downed) you keep only the tins; COLLECTED, they take 40% of your scrap and the bounty is paid. The cores learn from it (takedowns steel, perfects nerve, crates grease). Models in `tools/models/boarding.py`, view `src/view/boarding-view.ts` (procedural rigs in `src/view/rig.ts`), HUD and screen `src/screens/boarding.ts`, the choice `src/screens/board-choice.ts` (with a hull scan of her deck). `?crew=kestrel&screen=heist` arrives at one (the choice), `?crew=kestrel&screen=board` goes straight aboard (`&bounty=6` for the kelters soon, `&bot` lets a bot play).

## Feel: weapons, hits, shields, kills
- ~~Hull pips say nothing about what hit you, and +1 hull parts are lumpy.~~ Done: the hull is a number (Kestrel 60, Mule 100, the rest 80) shown as a bar notched every 20, what a hit bit off draining away after it. A hit takes what it's worth (`HIT_DMG` in `src/sim/level.ts`): a GRAZE 8 (spores, acid, jet wash), a HIT 15 (shots, shells, rams, the land), a HEAVY 30 (big orbs, torpedoes, seekers, slams, lightning, beams, a crate blowing), +30% a point of difficulty. A shield still stops a whole hit, so it's worth most against heavies; ARMOUR takes a flat amount off every hit (never under a third), so it's worth most against grazes. Armour Plate: +15 hull, armour 3. PLATED rolls +10–25% hull. The Weld mends 25%, a revive comes back at 40%, SECOND WIND leaves 10%. Off the field, a step is 20 hull (repair kits, the salvage patch, events, fuel burned, Lantern Mutual pays per 20 lost). A hit pops its damage, and the shake grows with the tier.
- ~~Player weapons look cartoonish.~~ Done: shots are hot rounds with light (`src/view/shots.ts`): white-hot slugs with tracer streaks, sparks of shot, rails with a twisting wake, little rockets, tumbling brass flak shells and limpet mines. Light parts leave no depth, so they get no ink outline. The ion beam is a shader strip with a white core, crawling edges and pulses. Muzzles spit sparks and the heavy guns puff smoke.
- ~~A hit sometimes feels like nothing happened.~~ Done: every hit bites (a white-hot flash and a hot bloom, sized by damage) and throws sparks back. Heavy hits and crits knock chips of hull off, puff smoke, hitstop 1–2 frames, shake the camera a little and pop the damage. An enemy that's been hit shows a slim health bar for a moment. Hurt enemies crackle with sparks.
- ~~Enemy shields look lame and absorb shots without any effect.~~ Done: shields are domes of hex cells (`src/view/shields.ts`), a front wedge for a Warden and a bubble for a closed-up boss. Each stopped shot sends a ripple across the dome, lights the cells there and glances off in sparks. The shield shatters into motes and glassy flakes when it drops. A closed-up boss now stops shots at its bubble instead of letting them pass.
- ~~Shot-down enemies should be spectacular.~~ Done: a kill adds a white-hot ball, petals of fire, two or three follow-up blasts, chunks of hull in its colours and drifting embers. The land below is lit for a moment (a small pool of point lights). The charred hull tumbles out of the sky trailing fire and smoke, then hits the ground in a second blast with a scorch mark, or lands in water in a column of spray. Ground units slump and burn where they stood.
